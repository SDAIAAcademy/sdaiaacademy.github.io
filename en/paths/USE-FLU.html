USE-FLU · Data & AI Fluency

Fluency for Everyone

Give every working adult the judgement and habits to use data and AI tools safely, check what they produce, and redesign their own work with them.

Who it is for
Staff in any role and sector, from new hires to managers, who use or will use dashboards, AI assistants and agents in routine work; no technical background is assumed and delivery is Arabic-first in the Kingdom.
Suggested order
Everyone starts with the baseline USE-FLU-101, and USE-FLU-102, USE-FLU-103 and USE-FLU-104 deepen data reading, how AI fails and safe use. USE-FLU-201 builds everyday collaboration with assistants and leads to USE-FLU-202 and the focused workshops USE-FLU-203 to USE-FLU-207, then to team redesign and adoption in USE-FLU-301 to USE-FLU-303.
