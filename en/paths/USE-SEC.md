# USE-SEC · AI in Sectors

Track: Data & AI Fluency

## Purpose

Show sector managers and specialists where AI creates value and risk in their sector, how to judge the evidence behind it and which rules apply, ending in an opportunity-and-risk map they can defend.

## Who it is for

Managers, specialists, regulators and advisers in health, energy, government, education, mobility, finance, justice, media, manufacturing, retail and telecommunications, agriculture and environment, and cities and tourism.

## Suggested order

Take USE-FLU-103 first, then the workshop for your sector (USE-SEC-201 to USE-SEC-212), which pairs with the matching function course in USE-PRO for individual use; advisers continue to LDS-ADV-201 and risk owners to LDS-AIG-204.

## Courses in order

In the order their prerequisites suggest.

### 1. [USE-SEC-201 · AI in Health Systems](../courses/USE-SEC-201.md)

- Level 200 · Apply · Workshop · 16 h · Available
- Needs: USE-FLU-103
- Distinguish AI products regulated as medical devices from administrative tools and explain what each requires.
- Compute predictive value, alert volume and missed cases for an imaging-triage tool at local prevalence.
- Analyse a population-health risk score for label choice and error by patient subgroup.
- Prepare a local validation and silent-trial plan for an ambient documentation tool.
- Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking health use cases by value, data readiness and risk tier.
