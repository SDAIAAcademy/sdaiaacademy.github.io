LDS-EXE · Executives & Leaders

Executive Leadership

Prepare executives to set direction for data and AI, fund, buy, scale and govern it, and redesign work around it, through decisions and artefacts they produce themselves after hands-on use of current systems.

Who it is for
Board members, executives and senior managers in public and private organisations, and the strategy, finance, procurement, people and risk staff who prepare their AI decisions.
Suggested order
Start with LDS-EXE-101, then LDS-EXE-201 for strategy and portfolio; take the decision courses LDS-EXE-202 to LDS-EXE-210 by role; LDS-EXE-301 and LDS-EXE-302 serve public-policy and talent leaders, and LDS-EXE-401 and LDS-EXE-402 are the cohort programmes for heads of AI and data offices.
