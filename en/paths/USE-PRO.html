USE-PRO · Data & AI Fluency

Fluency for Professions

Teach people in a given profession to use AI on their own function's tasks, recognise how it fails there, check its output by that profession's standards and follow the rules that govern their work.

Who it is for
Staff in public administration, human resources, finance, legal, communications, service, projects, research, health, sales, engineering and IT operations who already work with a general assistant and want to apply it safely to their own work.
Suggested order
Each course follows USE-FLU-201 (or USE-FLU-202) and stands alone; participants take the course for their function in USE-PRO-201 to USE-PRO-212, then USE-FLU-203 or USE-FLU-204 for depth and USE-FLU-301 to redesign a team workflow. Managers of a sector continue to the matching USE-SEC course.
