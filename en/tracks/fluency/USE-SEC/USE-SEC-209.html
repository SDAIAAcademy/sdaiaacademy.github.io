USE-SEC-209 AI in Manufacturing and Industry

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-SEC AI in Sectors

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of eight sessions with labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Knows how a production line, its quality checks and maintenance run; uses a spreadsheet

Description

Plant, quality and maintenance managers score inspection and maintenance models by error cost on public industrial data, audit a quality dataset for readiness, review a robotics-cell change for safety and build an opportunity-and-risk map for a synthetic plant.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Describe the data, labels and owners that inspection, maintenance, optimisation and robotics cells need.

    Given four synthetic plant deployments.

    Evidence a table matching the key.

  2. Applied

    Measure an inspection model's miss and false-reject rates and convert them into cost per thousand parts.

    Given 200 public defect images labelled in class.

    Evidence an error table and a chosen operating point.

  3. Applied

    Use a remaining-useful-life forecast to set a maintenance trigger and compare it with a fixed schedule.

    Given public run-to-failure engine data.

    Evidence a table of failures, early replacements and cost per policy.

  4. Applied

    Analyse a quality dataset for missing defect classes, label disagreement and sensor drift.

    Given a synthetic log of 20,000 parts.

    Evidence a data-readiness note.

  5. Performance

    Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking plant AI uses by value, data readiness, safety impact and effort to scale.

    Given a synthetic plant profile.

    Evidence a one-page map defended in five minutes.

Topics

Uses

  • Visual inspection and anomaly detection
  • Predictive maintenance
  • Process and energy optimisation
  • Robotics cells and digital twins

Quality data

  • Rare defects and imbalance
  • Inspector disagreement
  • Drift, shifts and line changes

Reading results

  • Miss versus false-reject rates
  • Operating points set by cost
  • Edge or cloud inference at line speed

Safety and security

  • Functional safety and reassessment after changes
  • Human stop authority
  • Operational technology security
  • Limits on monitoring workers

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Image labelling
Label Studio, CVAT
Notebook or spreadsheet
JupyterLite, Google Colab, LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel
Public industrial data
MVTec AD, NASA C-MAPSS

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off scenario check, ten cases (25%)
  • Lab records (35%)
  • Map and five-minute defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the AI-off check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the scenario check, labelling and the robotics-cell review. AI-tutor in the maintenance and quality labs. AI-full for the map, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
