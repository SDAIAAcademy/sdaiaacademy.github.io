USE-SEC-210 AI in Retail and Telecommunications

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-SEC AI in Sectors

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of eight sessions with labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Knows how a retailer or operator prices, sells and serves; uses a spreadsheet

Description

Commercial, marketing, network and customer managers test forecasts, churn targeting, pricing rules, recommender results and network alerts against baselines and experiments, check customer-data use and build an opportunity-and-risk map for a synthetic retailer or operator. Individual marketing work is USE-PRO-210.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Explain how recommenders, forecasts, pricing, churn and network models each turn data into a decision.

    Given a synthetic retailer and operator.

    Evidence decision chains checked against a key.

  2. Applied

    Compare a demand forecast with a naive seasonal baseline and state where the model earns its cost.

    Given two years of synthetic sales for 50 products.

    Evidence an error comparison by product group.

  3. Applied

    Analyse a churn-retention campaign by uplift and estimate retained revenue and wasted offers.

    Given a synthetic randomised trial.

    Evidence a targeting table and value estimate.

  4. Applied

    Test a personalised pricing rule for group differences and data used without consent.

    Given a synthetic customer file.

    Evidence a findings note with a proposed change.

  5. Applied

    Measure a network anomaly detector against confirmed incidents and set an alert threshold the team can handle.

    Given a week of synthetic cell-site alerts.

    Evidence a precision and recall table and a threshold.

  6. Performance

    Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking use cases by measured value, customer harm and data rights.

    Given a synthetic retailer or operator profile.

    Evidence a one-page map defended in five minutes.

Topics

Retail uses

  • Recommenders and search
  • Demand forecasting
  • Pricing and promotions

Telecommunications uses

  • Churn and next-best offer
  • Network anomalies and capacity
  • Energy saving in radio networks

Measuring value

  • Baselines before models
  • Offline metrics versus A/B tests
  • Uplift and incremental value
  • Feedback loops

Customer data

  • Consent for marketing and profiling
  • Vulnerable customers and personalised prices
  • Network location data

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Spreadsheet
LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets
Notebook running provided analyses
JupyterLite, Google Colab

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off scenario check, ten cases (25%)
  • Lab records (35%)
  • Map and five-minute defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the AI-off check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the scenario check and the first attempt at the uplift lab. AI-tutor in the other labs. AI-full for the map, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
