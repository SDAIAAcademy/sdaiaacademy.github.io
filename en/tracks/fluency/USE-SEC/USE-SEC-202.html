USE-SEC-202 AI in Energy and Petrochemicals

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-SEC AI in Sectors

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-103 How AI Systems Learn and Where They Fail
  • Works in or advises an energy operation

Description

Managers and engineers who decide where AI is safe and worth using work on a fictional integrated energy company with public and synthetic data. They test a load forecast against baselines, price maintenance and fault-detection false alarms and draft the AI part of a safety case, ending with a defended opportunity-and-risk map.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify each energy AI application as advisory, supervisory or closed-loop and the evidence it needs.

    Given ten applications.

    Evidence a table correct on eight rows.

  2. Applied

    Test a learned load forecast against seasonal-naive and persistence baselines.

    Given a provided forecast and two years of public hourly load.

    Evidence an error table and use recommendation.

  3. Applied

    Measure lead time, missed events and false alarms of maintenance and fault-detection models and price them.

    Given provided model outputs, public data and a cost table.

    Evidence a cost-of-errors sheet with a threshold.

  4. Applied

    Draft the AI part of a safety case covering hazards, operating envelope, override and fallback.

    Given an advisory system for a compressor train.

    Evidence a safety argument scored on a checklist.

  5. Performance

    Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking energy use cases by value, data readiness, control level and risk tier.

    Given the synthetic company.

    Evidence the map, defended before a mock safety committee.

Topics

Uses

  • Seismic and reservoir modelling
  • Soft sensors and process optimisation
  • Load and renewable forecasting
  • Predictive maintenance and fault detection

Methods and data

  • Naive baselines
  • Physics-informed and hybrid models
  • Rare failures and scarce labels
  • Historian data quality

Safety

  • Advisory to closed-loop control
  • Safety cases (depth in DEV-PHY-401)
  • Operational technology cybersecurity
  • Energy use of AI itself

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Public industrial data
NASA C-MAPSS turbofan data, Tennessee Eastman process data, published grid load data
No-code analysis
Orange Data Mining, KNIME, a spreadsheet

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off scenario check (20%)
  • Lab artefacts (35%)
  • Map (35%)
  • Panel defence (10%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the map; every control-loop use case names an override authority

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the scenario check; AI-tutor in labs; AI-full for the safety argument and map, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
