USE-SEC-211 AI in Agriculture, Water and Environment

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-SEC AI in Sectors

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of eight sessions with labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Knows how farms, water utilities or environment agencies monitor their work; uses a spreadsheet

Description

Agriculture, water and environment managers use public satellite imagery, open Earth-observation model outputs and synthetic sensor and meter data, check results against ground truth and build an opportunity-and-risk map for a synthetic water and agriculture authority.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Describe what optical, radar and thermal imagery, field sensors and reanalysis can measure for crops and water.

    Given eight monitoring questions.

    Evidence a matched table, at least six correct.

  2. Applied

    Compute a vegetation index time series and flag plots whose pattern disagrees with the declared crop.

    Given public imagery and synthetic plot declarations.

    Evidence a flagged list with field-check requests.

  3. Applied

    Measure a land-cover map's accuracy by class against ground-truth points.

    Given an open model's output and 200 points.

    Evidence an accuracy table and permitted uses.

  4. Applied

    Compare a forecast-driven irrigation schedule with a fixed one on water use and crop-stress days.

    Given a synthetic farm with sensor history.

    Evidence a comparison table stating uncertainty.

  5. Performance

    Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking uses by water saved, data readiness, equity and accountability.

    Given a synthetic authority profile.

    Evidence a one-page map defended in five minutes.

Topics

Uses

  • Crop and yield monitoring
  • Irrigation and water accounting
  • Leak detection
  • Drought, dust and heat warning

Earth-observation and sensor data

  • Resolution, revisit and cloud cover
  • Earth-observation foundation models
  • Ground truth and field campaigns

Climate services

  • Reanalysis, seasonal forecasts and AI weather models
  • Skill against climatology
  • Explaining uncertainty to farmers

Rights and equity

  • Farm data ownership and open licences
  • Small farms with sparse ground truth
  • Field checks before sanctions

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Imagery viewer and analysis
QGIS, Copernicus Browser, Google Earth Engine
Public imagery and climate data
Sentinel-2, ERA5 reanalysis
Open Earth-observation model (outputs provided)
Prithvi-EO, TerraMind
Spreadsheet
LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off scenario check, ten cases (25%)
  • Lab records (35%)
  • Map and five-minute defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the AI-off check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the scenario check and the accuracy check. AI-tutor in the imagery, irrigation and leak labs. AI-full for the map, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
