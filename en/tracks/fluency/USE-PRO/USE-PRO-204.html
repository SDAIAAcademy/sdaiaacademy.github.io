USE-PRO-204 AI for Legal, Regulatory and Compliance Work

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-PRO Fluency for Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Reads contracts, regulations or policies at work and knows how laws are cited locally

Description

Participants act as the legal team of a synthetic company engaging a data-processing supplier. With an assistant they research, review the contract against a playbook and map a regulation to obligations, checking every authority at source and deciding handling before documents enter a tool.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify how AI legal research fails: invented authorities, wrong jurisdiction, repealed provisions, misstated holdings.

    Given a memo with ten citations, four faulty.

    Evidence a check table catching all four, with sources.

  2. Applied

    Apply a contract playbook with an assistant to flag deviations, missing clauses and changes between drafts.

    Given a 25-page synthetic agreement, its earlier draft and a playbook.

    Evidence an issues list and comparison report checked against a reference.

  3. Applied

    Prepare an obligations register mapping provisions to owners, controls and evidence.

    Given one chapter of a public data-protection regulation.

    Evidence every entry citing an article verified in the official text.

  4. Applied

    Classify matter documents for confidentiality, privilege and personal data, and choose the tool and redaction each allows.

    Given twelve synthetic dispute documents.

    Evidence handling decisions marked against a key.

  5. Performance

    Redesign one recurring legal task and measure time and errors before and after.

    Given a pre-work baseline and two AI-assisted runs.

    Evidence a redesign card defended in a live review.

Topics

Where legal AI fails

  • Invented cases, articles and quotations
  • Agreeing with a leading question

Checking authorities and contracts

  • Legislation portals and consolidated texts
  • Authoritative versions and unofficial translations
  • Playbook positions, fallbacks and red lines

Regulatory mapping

  • From provision to obligation
  • Owners, controls and evidence

Confidentiality and privilege

  • Privilege and third-party tools
  • Redaction and pseudonymisation
  • Disclosing AI use to clients and courts

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Assistant approved for confidential work
an open-weight model in Open WebUI, an organisation-approved commercial assistant
Document comparison
LibreOffice compare, Microsoft Word compare
Official legal texts
the jurisdiction's legislation portal, regulators' publication pages

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check (20%)
  • Lab artefacts (40%)
  • Redesign card and live review (40%)
To pass
70% overall, with no unverified authority in any submitted artefact

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and handling decisions; AI-tutor for the playbook; AI-full in review, mapping and the redesign, with every authority checked.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
