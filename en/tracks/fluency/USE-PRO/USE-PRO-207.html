USE-PRO-207 AI for Project, Procurement and Operations Work

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-PRO Fluency for Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Works in projects, purchasing or operations and uses spreadsheets

Description

Participants use AI on project and purchasing paperwork for one synthetic facilities upgrade: a charter, meeting recordings, a risk log and three supplier proposals. They recheck an AI-drafted plan, trace meeting actions to the transcript, compute status from task data and score proposals against preset weights. Buying AI systems is LDS-EXE-205.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify AI drafting failures in project documents: invented owners and dates, lost dependencies, inflated status, wrong totals.

    Given five artefacts with ten planted errors.

    Evidence an error log flagging at least eight.

  2. Applied

    Prepare a project plan with an assistant and test its dependencies, durations and calendar.

    Given the case charter.

    Evidence a recomputed and reconciled critical path.

  3. Applied

    Use a meeting-to-action pipeline to turn recordings into decisions, owned actions and risk updates.

    Given three meetings in Arabic and English.

    Evidence an action register citing a transcript time for each entry.

  4. Applied

    Apply a weighted evaluation to supplier proposals, with AI extracting values and code computing scores.

    Given three proposals and preset criteria.

    Evidence a matrix with page references and each human judgement noted.

  5. Performance

    Redesign one of their own recurring tasks with AI and measure time and errors on two runs.

    Given the pre-work baseline.

    Evidence a redesign card with before-and-after figures, reviewed live.

Topics

Where AI fails here

  • Drafting versus deciding and signing
  • Polished output gets less checking
  • Low-quality output passed between colleagues

Plans, status and risks

  • Dependency and calendar checks
  • Status computed under written rules
  • Challenging an AI-drafted risk log

Meetings and suppliers

  • Actions traced and confirmed by owners
  • Scores computed in code, not by the model
  • Bid confidentiality and conflicts of interest

Measuring a task redesign

  • Baseline and delegation card
  • Comparing two runs

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

General-purpose assistant
a local open-weight model, the organisation's approved assistant
Meeting transcription
Whisper, an approved meeting platform
Scheduling and computation
ProjectLibre, OpenProject, a spreadsheet or notebook

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off check: correct an AI-drafted status report and price comparison (20%)
  • Labs (35%)
  • Redesign card (35%)
  • Live review (10%)
To pass
70% overall, and no figure the participant cannot recompute

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the error-finding lab. AI-tutor while planning: it questions dependencies but does not build the schedule. AI-full elsewhere, with every figure traced to source.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
