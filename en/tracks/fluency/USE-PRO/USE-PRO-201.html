USE-PRO-201 AI for Public Servants and Policy Work

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-PRO Fluency for Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Drafts official correspondence, briefs or minutes in current work

Description

A synthetic ministry is consulting the public on a new licensing rule. Participants do its drafting and analysis with an approved assistant, classifying material before it enters a tool and tracing every factual statement to an official source.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify AI drafting errors typical of government work: superseded rules, invented article numbers, misattributed positions.

    Given a brief with eight planted faults.

    Evidence six or more faults found, each with its settling source.

  2. Applied

    Classify case documents by sensitivity and match each to the tools its level allows.

    Given ten synthetic documents across four classification levels.

    Evidence a classification sheet and a sanitised prompt, marked against a key.

  3. Applied

    Prepare a consultation summary with theme counts that keeps minority positions and flags duplicate campaign comments.

    Given 300 synthetic comments in Arabic and English.

    Evidence theme counts agreeing with the participant's hand coding of 20 comments.

  4. Applied

    Draft an options brief and a citizen notice in which every factual statement cites an official source.

    Given a draft regulation, a statistics release and two circulars.

    Evidence both documents with a source column and no untraced claim.

  5. Performance

    Redesign one recurring task from the participant's work and measure time and errors before and after.

    Given a pre-work baseline and two AI-assisted runs.

    Evidence a redesign card defended in a live review.

Topics

Policy work and its AI failure modes

  • Superseded rules and unsourced figures
  • Summaries that drop minority views
  • Campaign comments and instructions hidden in submissions

Checking against official sources

  • Gazettes, legislation portals and statistics releases
  • Effective dates and consolidated texts

Government data rules

  • Classification levels and approved tools
  • Personal data in case files
  • Freedom of information and disclosure of AI use

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Assistant approved for official use
an open-weight model in Open WebUI, an organisation-approved commercial assistant
Source-grounded notebook
Open WebUI knowledge collections, NotebookLM
Spreadsheet
LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check (20%)
  • Lab artefacts (40%)
  • Redesign card and live review (40%)
To pass
70% overall, with no classified or personal detail entered into an unapproved tool

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and hand coding; AI-tutor while learning classification; AI-full in drafting and the redesign, which the participant defends.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
