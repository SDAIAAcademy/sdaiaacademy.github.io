USE-PRO-202 AI for Human Resources and Talent Work

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-PRO Fluency for Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Works in recruitment, talent, learning or employee relations, or manages those who do

Description

A synthetic organisation is hiring an analyst and planning team learning. Participants prepare adverts, interviews and learning plans with an assistant and test it for bias with matched curricula vitae (CVs), under one rule: AI helps prepare, people decide about people.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Explain why ranking candidates with a general assistant is high-risk and which HR decisions stay with people.

    Given six described HR uses of AI.

    Evidence a risk-tier table, five of six rows correct.

  2. Applied

    Analyse an AI-drafted job description for exclusionary wording and inflated requirements, and revise it.

    Given an occupational role card as the skills profile.

    Evidence a tracked-change revision with a reason per change.

  3. Applied

    Test a screening prompt for bias with applications that differ only in name, gender or age.

    Given 10 synthetic CV pairs, each scored twice.

    Evidence score shifts set against repeat-run variation, and a recommendation that follows.

  4. Applied

    Prepare an interview guide and learning plans without passing identifiable data to unapproved tools.

    Given three pseudonymised profiles.

    Evidence anchored questions, gap-based plans and a sanitisation log.

  5. Performance

    Redesign one recurring HR task and measure time, errors and fairness before and after.

    Given a pre-work baseline and two AI-assisted runs.

    Evidence a redesign card defended in a live review.

Topics

HR tasks and where AI fits

  • Decisions that stay with people
  • Candidates' AI use and unreliable detectors

Bias and screening risk

  • How bias enters through data, wording and instructions
  • Matched pairs and repeat runs
  • Employment as a high-risk use
  • Fairness in the redesign measure

Fair assessment and employee data

  • Job-related questions and anchored scales
  • Explaining and contesting decisions
  • Special-category employee records
  • Pseudonymising before prompting

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Assistant approved for HR use
an open-weight model in Open WebUI, an organisation-approved commercial assistant
Skills frameworks
the national occupational role cards, ESCO, O*NET
Spreadsheet
LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check (20%)
  • Lab artefacts (40%)
  • Redesign card and live review (40%)
To pass
70% overall, with the paired test completed and its recommendation supported

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and first-draft interview questions; AI-tutor for the revision; AI-full in the bias test, plans and redesign, which the participant defends.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
