USE-PRO-206 AI for Customer and Citizen Service Work

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-PRO Fluency for Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Handles customer or citizen enquiries in Arabic, English or both, or supervises people who do

Description

Participants staff a synthetic municipal service centre where an automated agent answers first and hands cases to people. They draft grounded replies, route escalations including impersonation attempts and sample a week of conversations for quality.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Distinguish grounded from ungrounded replies and identify their failures, such as invented policies and promises staff cannot keep.

    Given ten drafted replies, half grounded.

    Evidence faulty sentences marked, eight of ten labels correct.

  2. Applied

    Use a grounded assistant to draft courteous Arabic and English replies, checked against cited articles.

    Given 20 synthetic tickets and a knowledge base with two stale articles.

    Evidence replies with sources and a list of knowledge-base gaps.

  3. Applied

    Apply escalation rules to route complaints, vulnerable people, rights, safety, refunds above a limit and suspected impersonation.

    Given 30 tickets and agent handover transcripts.

    Evidence a routing log matching the key on 27 of 30.

  4. Applied

    Measure reply quality by sampling, rubric scoring and error rates by category.

    Given 500 synthetic conversations from one week.

    Evidence a double-marked random sample of 30 and the top three fixes.

  5. Performance

    Redesign one recurring service task and measure handling time, errors and escalations before and after.

    Given a pre-work baseline and two AI-assisted runs.

    Evidence a redesign card defended in a live review.

Topics

Service work beside AI

  • Handover from agent to person
  • What staff own: promises, exceptions, apologies

Grounding, tone and transparency

  • Answering only from the knowledge base
  • Stale articles and agreeing with a wrong premise
  • Courtesy and dialect in Arabic and English
  • Telling people when AI is involved

Escalation and fraud

  • Complaints, vulnerability, rights and safety
  • Impersonation and cloned voices

Quality sampling

  • Sampling plans, rubrics and second raters
  • Feeding fixes to the knowledge-base owner

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Assistant grounded in a document set
Open WebUI knowledge collections, a service platform's reply-drafting feature
Help-desk sandbox
Zammad, osTicket
Spreadsheet
LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check (20%)
  • Lab artefacts (40%)
  • Redesign card and live review (40%)
To pass
70% overall, with every high-risk ticket escalated correctly

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and first routing lab; AI-tutor while learning the rubric; AI-full in drafting and the redesign, with each reply checked against its article.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
