USE-PRO-208 AI for Researchers and Scientific Work

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-PRO Fluency for Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • USE-FLU-201 Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own
  • Has worked on a research project and can run a provided notebook

Description

Researchers in any discipline use AI on one synthetic project, a scoping question on heat exposure in outdoor workers with 200 retrieved records and a public dataset, from screening and summary checks to a reproducible analysis and a disclosure statement. Research practice for AI scientists is SPC-RSP-301.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Distinguish acceptable, disclosable and prohibited research uses of AI.

    Given twelve uses, from language editing to reviewing a confidential manuscript.

    Evidence a table matching the reference on ten.

  2. Applied

    Use scholarly databases and an AI screening assistant to map a literature with a recorded search.

    Given a scoping question and 200 records.

    Evidence a search log and the assistant's recall against the participant's screening of 50 records.

  3. Applied

    Test AI-written paper summaries for misreported populations, methods, effect sizes and conclusions.

    Given ten summaries with planted errors.

    Evidence an audit citing the contradicting passage for each error.

  4. Applied

    Implement an AI-assisted analysis from a pre-stated plan and reproduce it from a clean run.

    Given a public dataset.

    Evidence a notebook that re-runs cleanly, with a decision log.

  5. Performance

    Redesign one research task with AI, measure it on two runs and disclose the AI use.

    Given the pre-work baseline.

    Evidence a redesign card and a disclosure meeting a named journal policy, reviewed live.

Topics

Integrity and disclosure

  • Misconduct rules applied to AI
  • Disclosure rules
  • Confidential manuscripts and reviews

Literature mapping

  • Search strategies across databases
  • Screening recall and stopping rules
  • Checking references and retractions

Analysis and writing

  • Plan before prompt
  • Forking paths at machine speed
  • Drafting only from own outputs

Data and participants

  • Sensitive and government data
  • Ethics approval conditions

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Scholarly search and metadata
OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed
Screening and references
ASReview, Zotero, JabRef
Analysis notebook
Jupyter, R with Quarto

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off check: find errors in two AI summaries (20%)
  • Labs (35%)
  • Redesign card and disclosure (35%)
  • Live review (10%)
To pass
70% overall, and no unverified reference or untraceable number

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the classification lab. AI-tutor for the analysis plan: it questions the design, never writes it. AI-full elsewhere, with every use recorded for disclosure.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
