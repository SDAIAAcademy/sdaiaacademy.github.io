USE-EDU-102 Learning with AI without Losing the Skill

  • Level 100 · Explain
  • Primer
  • 4 h
  • Audience: beginner

Track

Data & AI Fluency

Stream

USE-EDU Learners and Educators

Level

Level 100 · Explain

Audience

beginner

Format

Primer: a half-day, facilitated or self-paced, with a scenario test

Total hours

4 h

Prerequisites

  • None

Description

Learners see trial evidence that open assistants lower later unaided performance while a hint-only tutor does not. They compare answer and tutor modes, sort interaction patterns, set an assistant to tutor, and plan two weeks of AI-off-first practice with retrieval.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Distinguish interaction patterns that build understanding from those that replace it, citing trial evidence.

    Given eight anonymised transcripts and three trial summaries.

    Evidence a sorted set with reasons, scored against a key.

  2. Applied

    Configure an assistant to give one hint at a time, ask for explain-back and withhold final answers.

    Given problems from the learner's course or work.

    Evidence instructions and a transcript with no final answer.

  3. Applied

    Prepare a two-week practice plan with AI-off first attempts, retrieval and spacing.

    Given the learner's own timetable.

    Evidence the plan, with its first AI-off session done in the course.

Topics

The evidence

  • Practice gains versus unaided performance
  • Why effortful practice feels worse

Patterns that help and harm

  • Delegation, repeated AI debugging
  • Conceptual questions, generate-then-explain
  • Polished answers get checked less

Tutor mode

  • Study modes and their limits
  • Retrieval, spacing, disclosure

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

AI assistant
an open-weight model run locally, an approved assistant's study mode
Spaced-repetition flashcards
Anki, paper flashcards

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off scenario test, 12 items (60%)
  • Tutor transcript and plan (40%)
To pass
70% overall

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the unaided variants and the test; AI-tutor elsewhere; no AI-full.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
