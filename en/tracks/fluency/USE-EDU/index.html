Stream

USE-EDU Learners and Educators

Purpose
Help students learn with data and AI without losing their own skill, and help educators teach, assess and configure tutors for learners who all have assistants.
Who it is for
University students in any discipline, learners in training programmes, and the faculty, school teachers and trainers who teach and assess them; no technical background is assumed.
Suggested order
Students take USE-EDU-101, with USE-EDU-102 as a short companion for any learner. Educators start with USE-EDU-201; university faculty add USE-EDU-202 to deliver the national curriculum, school teachers take USE-EDU-203, and USE-EDU-204 follows for anyone who wants a tested course tutor.
