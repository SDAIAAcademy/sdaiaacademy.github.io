SPC-RSP-301 Research Practice Studio

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Studio
  • 60 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Data Science

Stream

SPC-RSP Research Practice and Frontier

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Studio: eight weeks part-time with weekly coached clinics, an exchanged peer review and a final defence

Total hours

60 h

Prerequisites

  • Trains, evaluates and debugs a neural network or gradient-boosted model in Python, as in SPC-DLR-302 or SPC-MOD-201
  • Computes a confidence interval and runs a two-sample test, as in FND-MTH-104 or SPC-STA-201
  • Uses Git for daily work

Description

Participants practise a research group's working habits on one small question: structured paper reading, reproducing a published result on a small budget, a tracked follow-up experiment across seeds, sound statistics, a short paper, a peer review and an open-source contribution. They also supervise a research agent running overnight experiments and decide which of its results to accept.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare a reading note that states a paper's claim, evidence, baselines, compute and threats to validity.

    Given two recent papers.

    Evidence two reading notes scored against a rubric.

  2. Performance

    Reproduce one figure or table from a published paper within a stated compute budget and explain any gap.

    Given the paper, any released code and a fixed accelerator allocation.

    Evidence a repository with configurations, seeds, run logs and a gap analysis.

  3. Performance

    Design and run a tracked follow-up experiment with a stated hypothesis, controls and a seed count justified by a power estimate.

    Given the reproduced baseline.

    Evidence a plan dated before the runs and a results table with intervals.

  4. Applied

    Compute the uncertainty of model comparisons across seeds and data splits, correcting for multiple comparisons.

    Given run logs with at least five seeds per condition.

    Evidence a statistics appendix with paired intervals and corrected tests.

  5. Performance

    Review a peer's short paper for soundness, clarity, reproducibility and ethics.

    Given a form modelled on a major conference's checklist.

    Evidence a written review rated by the coach.

  6. Performance

    Audit a research agent's overnight experiment results, deciding for each whether to accept, rerun or reject it.

    Given a sandboxed agent that may edit one training file.

    Evidence an audit log with a decision and a reason for every result.

Topics

Reading and positioning

  • Three-pass reading and reading notes
  • Related work and citation checks
  • Claims, evidence and threats to validity

Reproduction and experiment design

  • Configurations, seeds and environment capture
  • Compute budgets; debugging on a CPU first
  • Baselines, controls and ablations

Tracking and statistics

  • Experiment tracking and run metadata
  • Seed variance, paired comparisons, bootstrap intervals
  • Multiple comparisons and forking paths

Supervising research agents

  • Automated experiment loops and what they optimise
  • Overfitting to the validation set by search
  • Audit trails and acceptance rules

Writing, review and open science

  • Paper structure, figures, compute reporting and limitations
  • Reviewing against a reproducibility and ethics checklist
  • Authorship, AI disclosure, fabricated citations
  • Contributing to open-source research code

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Deep learning framework
PyTorch, JAX
Experiment tracking
MLflow, TensorBoard, Weights & Biases
Research agent in a sandbox
an open-source autonomous experiment loop, a coding agent restricted to one workspace
Statistics
SciPy, statsmodels
Compute
a laptop CPU for debugging, an Academy-provided accelerator allocation

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised oral check on experiment design and statistics, without AI (15%)
  • Reproduction repository and gap analysis (25%)
  • Agent audit log (10%)
  • Paper and peer review (25%)
  • Defence (25%)
To pass
70% overall, at least 60% on the defence, and every reported number rerunnable from the repository

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the first reading note (written in the clinic), the peer review and the oral check. AI-tutor in the reproduction and statistics labs. AI-full, research agents included, for the follow-up experiment and paper, with all use disclosed.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
