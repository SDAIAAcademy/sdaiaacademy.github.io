DS-390 Data Science Practicum

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Practicum
  • 100 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data Science

Stream

DS-300 Data Science: courses that help prepare for the Associate level

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Practicum: about eight weeks part-time, teams of three or four on a decision question set by a sponsoring government entity or company, with milestone reviews by experienced data scientists

Total hours

100 h

Prerequisites

  • DS-309 Responsible Data Science: Privacy, Fairness and Documentation
  • DS-310 Problem Framing and Analytical Review
  • At least four further level 300 core courses, including DS-302 or DS-303
  • About ten hours a week for eight weeks

Badge

Helps prepare for the SDAIA Data Science badge, Associate level.

Description

Teams of three or four take one decision question from a sponsoring government entity or company, such as which cases to inspect first or whether a pilot worked, and carry it from framing to a recommendation the sponsor can act on: decision and metrics, data audit, an analysis plan fixed before outcomes are seen, validated analysis with stated uncertainty, and a briefing. Data are the sponsor's anonymised extract or a synthetic replica; when neither is approved in time, the team works on a prepared case from Sidra Freight, a fictional logistics company.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare a framing memo that states a sponsor's request as a decision question with options, a success metric and constraints.

    Given a brief and two stakeholder interviews.

    Evidence a framing memo accepted by the sponsor.

  2. Applied

    Prepare a data audit covering provenance, quality, classification, personal data and fitness for the question.

    Given the extract and its sharing terms.

    Evidence an audit and handling plan approved by the reviewer.

  3. Performance

    Design an analysis plan matched to the question type and fix it before outcomes are examined.

    Given the framing memo and the audited data.

    Evidence a dated plan naming methods, validation and stopping rules.

  4. Performance

    Build and validate the analysis with baselines, sensitivity checks and stated uncertainty for every number the recommendation uses.

    Given a shared team repository.

    Evidence a pipeline that reruns from raw extract to final figures.

  5. Performance

    Defend a recommendation, its limits and the evidence that would reverse it to the sponsor and a technical panel.

    Given a 20-minute briefing and questions.

    Evidence the decision pack and panel scores on a published rubric.

  6. Applied

    Prepare a hand-over pack with a decision log, an analysis card and open risks.

    Given the sponsor's operating team as readers.

    Evidence a pack accepted by the sponsor.

Topics

Framing and data

  • Stakeholder interviews and success metrics
  • Sharing terms, classification and anonymisation
  • Fitness of data for the question

Analysis design

  • Choosing a descriptive, predictive, causal or experimental design
  • Plans and validation schemes fixed in advance

Delivery under review

  • Milestone and code reviews
  • AI-assisted steps with verification notes

Recommendation and hand-over

  • Briefing a sponsor and answering challenge
  • Decision logs, analysis cards and monitoring

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Analysis environment
Python with Jupyter or marimo and DuckDB, R with Quarto
Data validation
Pandera, Great Expectations
De-identification and synthetic replicas
ARX, Synthetic Data Vault
Version control and review
Git with GitLab, Git with GitHub
Language model assistant approved for the sponsor's data
an open-weight model served locally, an approved hosted model

How the work is assessed

  • Individual supervised check without AI assistance across the seven domains in week 8 (10%)
  • Milestone reviews by experienced data scientists, on a rubric (25%)
  • Decision pack and repository on a published rubric (35%)
  • Panel defence with individual questions (30%)
To pass
70% overall, at least 60% in the individual part of the defence, and every milestone accepted

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the individual supervised check and the defence. AI-full in the project with a model the sponsor has approved for its data, with a verification note for each assisted step.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
