Prerequisites

  • DS-204 Machine Learning for Tabular Data: Baselines and Validation

Badge

Helps prepare for the SDAIA Data Science badge, Associate level.

Description

Participants produce the evidence an independent validator needs about a trained model: whether its probabilities can be trusted, how wide its errors are, what drives its predictions, where it stops working and whom it fails. For Mizan Community Lending, a fictional microfinance lender, they repair calibration, wrap models in conformal intervals and prediction sets, test coverage by subgroup and under shift, show where explanations mislead, tabulate subgroup error and write a validation report against a published checklist. Fairness mitigation and documentation are DS-309.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Measure calibration with proper scores and reliability diagrams, recalibrate, and report the effect by subgroup.

    Given a trained default classifier and a held-out set.

    Evidence before-and-after diagrams, Brier scores and a subgroup table.

  2. Applied

    Implement conformal intervals and prediction sets and test coverage overall, by subgroup and under simulated shift.

    Given a loss-amount regression and a three-grade risk classifier.

    Evidence a table of target against achieved coverage with widths and set sizes.

  3. Applied

    Apply permutation, accumulated-local-effect and Shapley-value explanations and test their stability across refits.

    Given a boosted model with two correlated income features.

    Evidence an explanation report with a stability panel and one documented misleading result.

  4. Performance

    Diagnose why a model degrades on a later batch from slice metrics, coverage loss and changed explanations.

    Given a shifted batch with a seeded cause.

    Evidence a memo naming the cause and an action.

  5. Performance

    Audit a model package for conceptual soundness, outcome performance and monitoring readiness and decide whether it is fit for use.

    Given another team's model, documentation and data, with a reporting checklist and the national bias guide.

    Evidence a validation report with rated findings, subgroup gaps and conditions of use, defended before a panel.

Topics

What validation must show

  • Conceptual soundness, outcome analysis and monitoring
  • Guarantees and assumptions
  • Checklists and model cards

Calibration

  • Proper scoring rules
  • Reliability diagrams
  • Recalibration
  • Calibration by subgroup

Conformal prediction

  • Exchangeability
  • Split conformal intervals
  • Prediction sets
  • Coverage by group and under shift

Explanations and their limits

  • Permutation importance
  • Partial dependence and accumulated local effects
  • Shapley values
  • Correlated features

Subgroups, shift and conditions of use

  • Subgroup performance as validation evidence
  • Diagnosing degradation
  • Conditions of use and human review

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off entry and exit checks on calibration, coverage and explanation limits (20%)
  • Capstone rubric: calibration and coverage 25, explanations 20, subgroup and shift findings 25, decision 20, reproducibility 10 (60%)
  • Panel defence (20%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on both the capstone rubric and the supervised check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for supervised checks and first readings of diagrams and coverage tables. AI-tutor in labs, explaining without writing code or findings. AI-full in the capstone, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
