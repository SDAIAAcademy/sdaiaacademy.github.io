SPC-MOD-305 Clustering, Anomaly Detection and Dimensionality Reduction

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data Science

Stream

SPC-MOD Machine Learning and Modelling

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days with labs, guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • SPC-MOD-201 Machine Learning for Tabular Data: Baselines and Validation
  • Uses vectors, distances and matrix notation

Description

Participants find structure in unlabelled data and show whether it is real. They reduce dimensions with principal components and nonlinear embeddings and see how projections mislead, segment with several clustering families, and validate clusters by internal indices, resampling stability and review with domain owners. They detect anomalies in synthetic transaction and sensor data and set alert thresholds against an investigation budget. Production drift monitoring is DEV-OPS-301.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Apply principal components and a nonlinear embedding and test which structures survive changes of settings and seeds.

    Given synthetic data with known structure.

    Evidence a notebook with a stability panel and artefacts named.

  2. Applied

    Apply centroid, mixture and density-based clustering under different scaling choices and measure internal indices and bootstrap stability.

    Given synthetic service-usage data.

    Evidence a comparison table and the chosen solution.

  3. Applied

    Measure the precision of anomaly detectors at a fixed investigation budget.

    Given synthetic card transactions with seeded fraud.

    Evidence a results table for three detectors.

  4. Performance

    Evaluate a segmentation delivered by another team and decide whether it is stable and useful enough to act on.

    Given the segmentation and its data.

    Evidence a one-page validation memo.

  5. Performance

    Design an anomaly-monitoring rule for a data feed with thresholds, alert budget and a review step.

    Given a year of daily metrics with seeded incidents.

    Evidence a design and a backtest of detected and missed incidents.

Topics

Reducing dimensions

  • Principal components and loadings
  • Nonlinear embeddings and their distortions

Clustering

  • Distance and scaling choices, including mixed data types
  • Centroid, mixture and density-based methods

Validation without labels

  • Internal indices
  • Stability under resampling
  • Usefulness checks with domain owners

Anomalies and monitoring

  • Median-based and seasonal baselines
  • Isolation forests and local outlier factors
  • Alert budgets and precision at k

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Unsupervised learning
scikit-learn, tidyclust
Nonlinear embedding
umap-learn, scikit-learn t-SNE
Anomaly detection
PyOD, scikit-learn outlier detectors

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check on projections and cluster indices (20%)
  • Graded labs (40%)
  • Capstone proposal (40%)
To pass
65% overall and at least 60% on the capstone

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and the first lab. AI-tutor in other labs. AI-full in the capstone, defended in the review.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
