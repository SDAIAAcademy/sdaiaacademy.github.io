SPC-MOD-402 Simulation and Agent-Based Modelling

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Data Science

Stream

SPC-MOD Machine Learning and Modelling

Level

Level 400 · Evaluate

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: two days with labs, guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • SPC-STA-201 Statistical Inference by Simulation
  • Writes Python classes and fits probability distributions to data

Description

Participants build a discrete-event model of a service centre and an agent-based model of how people adopt a new public service, validate both, calibrate unknown parameters against observed patterns, and compare policies through scenario experiments with replications and sensitivity analysis. Starter models are provided, so lab time goes to experiments and validation.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Performance

    Build a discrete-event model of a queueing service and compare staffing policies with replications and common random numbers.

    Given synthetic arrival and service logs.

    Evidence a results table with intervals for waiting time and utilisation per policy.

  2. Performance

    Build an agent-based model of adoption and document it with a standard protocol.

    Given a behavioural description and a synthetic social network.

    Evidence a runnable model with an Overview, Design concepts and Details (ODD) description.

  3. Performance

    Calibrate model parameters against observed aggregate patterns and report their uncertainty.

    Given synthetic adoption curves and parameter ranges.

    Evidence accepted parameter sets with fit diagnostics.

  4. Applied

    Analyse which inputs drive outputs with designed experiments and global sensitivity analysis.

    Given a calibrated model and a run budget.

    Evidence sensitivity indices and a ranked list of inputs worth measuring better.

  5. Performance

    Evaluate scenarios for a policy decision and defend what the model can and cannot support.

    Given three candidate interventions.

    Evidence a scenario brief with ranges, validation evidence and limits, defended live.

Topics

Choosing an approach

  • Discrete-event, agent-based and system dynamics models
  • When plain Monte Carlo is enough
  • Optimisation is in SPC-MOD-304; physical twins in DEV-PHY-301

Building the models

  • Entities, resources, queues and input modelling
  • Warm-up, run length and replications
  • Agents, rules, networks and emergence
  • Language-model agents as simulated people and their limits

Calibration and validation

  • Verification against analytic cases
  • Pattern-oriented and predictive validation
  • Approximate Bayesian computation and history matching

Scenario analysis

  • Experimental designs over parameters
  • Global sensitivity analysis

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Discrete-event simulation
SimPy, simmer for R
Agent-based modelling
Mesa, NetLogo
Sensitivity analysis and calibration
SALib, pyABC, a hand-written rejection sampler

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check on output analysis and validation (20%)
  • Graded labs (30%)
  • Capstone with live review (50%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the capstone

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the first model-building lab. AI-tutor in the calibration and sensitivity labs. AI-full in the capstone, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
