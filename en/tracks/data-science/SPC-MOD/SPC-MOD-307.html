SPC-MOD-307 Problem Framing, Product Sense and Analytical Review

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data Science

Stream

SPC-MOD Machine Learning and Modelling

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days with labs, guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • SPC-ANL-201 Exploratory Analysis and Visualisation
  • Has completed an analysis that a colleague or client used
  • Tells a randomised comparison from an observational one, as in SPC-EXP-201

Description

Participants practise the work before and after an analysis. They turn vague requests into decision questions with an estimand, a metric and a decision rule, map who decides and who is affected, and test whether data and deadline make the question answerable. Then they review others' analyses, including assistant-generated ones, and write notes a colleague can act on. Delegating analysis to assistants is SPC-ANL-202; briefing decision makers is SPC-ANL-301.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare a decision question with estimand, population, metric and decision rule from a request.

    Given five vague requests from synthetic cases.

    Evidence framing cards a peer can plan from unaided.

  2. Applied

    Analyse a metric set for proxy failure and gaming, and propose guardrail metrics.

    Given a metric tree for a service-improvement programme.

    Evidence an annotated tree with two guardrails and the behaviour each prevents.

  3. Performance

    Design an analysis plan stating data, method, threats to validity and the result that would change the decision.

    Given a framed question, a data inventory and a two-week deadline.

    Evidence a two-page plan scored against a rubric.

  4. Performance

    Review an analysis for errors in question fit, data handling, method, inference and communication.

    Given three seeded-flaw notebooks, one assistant-generated.

    Evidence review notes ranking the critical flaws by consequence, with fixes.

  5. Performance

    Decide whether a request needs description, an experiment, a causal study, a model or no analysis.

    Given a role-played stakeholder whose constraints change.

    Evidence a recommendation defended against the assessor's challenge.

Topics

From request to question

  • Decision, estimand and population
  • Descriptive, predictive and causal questions
  • CRISP-DM and its limits

Stakeholders and product sense

  • Decision makers, users and affected groups
  • The evidence each will accept
  • Value, cost of delay and reversibility

Metrics

  • Metric trees and proxies
  • Goodhart's law and guardrails
  • Definitions others can recompute

Planning and review

  • Feasibility and scoping to a deadline
  • A review checklist from question to conclusion
  • Spot-checking assistant-generated analyses

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Notebook or report
Jupyter, marimo, Quarto
Diagrams for metric trees
Mermaid, draw.io

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off framing exercise (20%)
  • Graded labs (30%)
  • Capstone with live session (50%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the capstone

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the framing exercise and first lab. AI-tutor in the metric and planning labs. AI-full in the review lab and capstone, where checking assistant output is assessed.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
