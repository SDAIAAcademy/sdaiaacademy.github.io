DS-490 Data Science Capstone: Defended Analysis

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Studio
  • 60 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Data Science

Stream

DS-400 Data Science: courses that help prepare for the Expert level

Level

Level 400 · Evaluate

Audience

specialist

Format

Studio: coached individual project over about eight weeks part-time, with milestone reviews, a peer review and a panel defence

Total hours

60 h

Prerequisites

  • DS-401 Bayesian Modelling and Decisions under Uncertainty
  • DS-403 Statistical Evaluation of AI Systems
  • DS-407 Leading Analytical Work: Review, Standards and Stakeholders
  • Has completed two further level 400 courses, or can show an analysis they led from question to recommendation

Badge

Helps prepare for the SDAIA Data Science badge, Expert level.

Description

Each participant takes one decision question from the case bank of the fictional Al-Arid Regional Development Authority (school transport, clinic opening hours, a small-business grant scheme, ambulance standby locations), or an approved, anonymised question from their employer, and carries it from framing to a defended recommendation. The analysis has a design fixed before outcomes are seen, uncertainty and sensitivity, validation and a responsible-practice section. A coach reviews it at four milestones, a peer reviews the draft and a panel examines the result. Credit goes to the reasoning, not to a favourable answer.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Performance

    Design a decision-grade analysis from a sponsor's question, fixing the estimand, decision rule and analysis plan before outcome data are examined.

    Given a sponsor brief and a data inventory.

    Evidence a time-stamped plan approved at the second milestone.

  2. Performance

    Diagnose data quality problems and their effect on the conclusion, and document the prepared data.

    Given case data with known and unannounced defects.

    Evidence a quality report and a datasheet.

  3. Performance

    Deliver the main analysis with a design matched to the question, whether experimental, quasi-experimental, model-based or Bayesian, and validate it.

    Given the approved plan.

    Evidence a repository that re-runs end to end and reproduces every reported number.

  4. Performance

    Quantify the uncertainty of the conclusion and test which assumptions would reverse the recommendation.

    Given the main estimate and the threats named in the plan.

    Evidence a sensitivity section with intervals and the break-point of each key assumption.

  5. Performance

    Audit the analysis for privacy, subgroup effects and limits under the national rules.

    Given the data's classification level and the groups the decision affects.

    Evidence a responsible-practice section with a sharing decision and subgroup results.

  6. Performance

    Defend the recommendation and its limits before a panel, re-running one result live.

    Given a 20-minute defence with questions.

    Evidence panel scores on a published rubric.

Topics

Framing and planning

  • Decision questions, estimands and decision rules
  • Plans fixed before outcomes
  • Scoping to eight weeks

Evidence quality

  • Data quality and its effect on conclusions
  • Validation matched to the design
  • Sensitivity and break-points

Responsible practice

  • Classification, minimisation and sharing
  • Subgroup effects
  • Disclosure of AI assistance

Reporting and defence

  • Decision briefs for non-specialists
  • Reproducible repositories
  • Answering a panel

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Analysis language and notebooks
Python with Jupyter or marimo, R with Quarto
Data engine
DuckDB, Polars, PostgreSQL
Statistical and causal modelling
statsmodels, PyMC, linearmodels, fixest for R
Reproducible environment and version control
uv with a lock file, containers, GitHub or GitLab

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check. AI-full during the project, with every assisted step disclosed in the repository and verified; in the defence the participant explains any part of the work without assistance.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
