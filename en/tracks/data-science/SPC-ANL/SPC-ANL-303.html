SPC-ANL-303 Business Intelligence Platform Practice: Governance, Performance and Enablement

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data Science

Stream

SPC-ANL Analysis and Business Intelligence

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days with labs, pre-work and a panel defence

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • SPC-ANL-203 Metrics, Semantic Models and Dashboards
  • Has published a dashboard on a BI platform

Description

Participants run a business intelligence (BI) platform as a governed service. They write and test measures in a BI calculation language, tune a slow report from its execution plan, and set up row-level access tested with personas. They design an executive scorecard and a certification, retirement and enablement plan for a sprawling report estate. Labs use an open-source platform or a free commercial edition.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Implement measures with period comparisons, semi-additive balances, ratios and filter-dependent logic.

    Given a semantic model and ten measure specifications.

    Evidence a test sheet with every measure matching a reference SQL result.

  2. Applied

    Configure row-level and object-level access rules and test them with personas.

    Given an access matrix with a restricted data class.

    Evidence a test log of what each persona sees.

  3. Performance

    Optimise a slow report using execution plans, indexes, aggregation tables and refresh design.

    Given a report missing its load-time target.

    Evidence before-and-after timings that meet the target.

  4. Applied

    Prepare an executive scorecard with objectives, measures, targets, owners and review rhythm.

    Given a strategy summary and a metric catalogue.

    Evidence a scorecard reviewed by a panel.

  5. Performance

    Design a governance and enablement plan covering certification, retirement, training and usage measures.

    Given two hundred reports with usage data.

    Evidence retire, merge or certify decisions and a measured enablement programme.

Topics

Calculation languages

  • How filters change a measure's result
  • Period comparisons and semi-additive measures
  • Testing against reference queries

Performance

  • Execution plans and indexes
  • Aggregations and incremental refresh
  • Cached extracts, live queries and model size

Access and scorecards

  • Row-level rules and personas
  • Classification-driven sharing and export
  • Leading and lagging measures, targets, owners

Governance and enablement

  • Certified, shared and personal content
  • Versioning and promotion to production
  • Usage analytics and report sprawl
  • AI assistants grounded in certified content
  • Training, champions and office hours

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

BI platform
Apache Superset, Metabase, Power BI, Tableau
BI calculation language
SQL measures in Cube Core, DAX, MDX
Analytical database
PostgreSQL, DuckDB
Version control for BI content
Git, platform deployment pipelines

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check: writing and debugging measures (25%)
  • Graded labs (30%)
  • Capstone and panel defence (45%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the panel defence

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised measure check. AI-tutor in the labs. AI-full in the capstone, which is defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
