SPC-STA-302 Survey, Sampling and Measurement Design

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Data Science

Stream

SPC-STA Statistics and Inference

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days with labs, guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • SPC-STA-201 Statistical Inference by Simulation
  • Fits and reads a logistic regression

Description

Participants design a survey that can answer its question and analyse one honestly. They choose a stratified or cluster design from a frame, compute design effects and design-based standard errors, weight for non-response and to population margins, and critique, pretest and check the reliability of questionnaire items. The workshop ends with a defended sampling and measurement plan.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Compute design weights, design effects and design-based standard errors for a stratified cluster sample.

    Given a synthetic household survey.

    Evidence estimates matching the reference.

  2. Applied

    Apply non-response adjustment and raking to population margins and measure the effect on estimates.

    Given differential non-response and published totals.

    Evidence a weighting log with before-and-after estimates.

  3. Applied

    Analyse questionnaire items for wording faults, scale reliability and evidence of validity.

    Given a draft questionnaire with seeded faults and synthetic pilot responses.

    Evidence an item critique, reliability estimates and a decision per item.

  4. Performance

    Review two disagreeing survey results and trace the gap to frame, non-response, wording or denominator.

    Given two public reports with method notes.

    Evidence a one-page review memo.

  5. Performance

    Design a sampling and measurement plan with frame, design, sample size, weighting and pretest.

    Given a brief, a budget and available frames.

    Evidence a design defended in review.

Topics

Frames and designs

  • Total survey error and coverage
  • Stratified, cluster and multi-stage designs
  • Design effect and sample size
  • Linearisation and replicate-weight variance

Weighting

  • Response propensity adjustment
  • Raking and calibration
  • Opt-in panels and limits of adjustment

Measurement

  • Wording, order and response scales
  • Cognitive pretesting and mode effects
  • Reliability and validity

Language models in survey work

  • Model coding of open answers as measurement (see SPC-ANL-204)
  • Why simulated respondents are not a sample

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Design-based survey analysis
R survey and srvyr, samplics for Python
Questionnaire authoring and collection
LimeSurvey, KoboToolbox
Reliability and agreement statistics
R psych, pingouin for Python

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check: compute a design effect and critique three items (25%)
  • Graded labs (35%)
  • Capstone plan and live review (40%)
To pass
70% overall and a pass on the capstone review

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and the questionnaire critique. AI-tutor in the other labs. AI-full in the defended capstone.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
