DEV-EVL-304 Annotation, Golden Data and Human Review Operations

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

AI Engineering

Stream

DEV-EVL Evaluation and Quality Engineering for AI Systems

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days with labs and a short design project, with guided pre-work

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • Computes proportions, confusion matrices and confidence intervals
  • Has evaluated a model or application with labelled data

Description

Participants write a labelling guideline, pilot it on synthetic Arabic and English responses, measure agreement and adjudicate, then build a versioned golden set, test whether a model labeller is reliable enough to share the work, and design and cost a review queue.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Draft a labelling guideline with definitions, decision rules and edge cases, and revise it after a pilot.

    Given two 60-response pilot rounds, three raters each.

    Evidence a versioned guideline whose changes cite measured disagreements.

  2. Applied

    Compute chance-corrected agreement by category and rater to find ambiguous categories.

    Given the pilot labels.

    Evidence an agreement report with intervals and actions.

  3. Applied

    Measure a language model labeller's reliability against adjudicated labels before and after a prompt change.

    Given two labeller configurations.

    Evidence a reliability report by category, with versions recorded.

  4. Performance

    Design a golden evaluation set with a sampling plan, adjudication, versioning and leakage safeguards.

    Given 400 labelled responses and the failure modes to cover.

    Evidence golden set version 1 with a datasheet.

  5. Performance

    Design and cost a human review queue: sampling rules, capacity, quality checks and a route into the evaluation set.

    Given a synthetic traffic profile and a review budget.

    Evidence a queue specification and a simulated week of load.

Topics

Guidelines and raters

  • Label types: binary, categorical, span, pairwise
  • Decision rules and versions
  • Rater training and qualification

Agreement and adjudication

  • Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's alpha
  • Disagreement as signal; adjudication methods

Golden data and model labellers

  • Stratified sampling for rare failure modes
  • Separation from training and prompts
  • Model labellers: reliability and drift

Review operations

  • Random, risk-targeted and low-confidence sampling
  • Gold items, spot audits, cost per item
  • Rater welfare and dialect expertise

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Annotation tool
Label Studio, a spreadsheet with locked validation rules
Agreement statistics
statsmodels, scikit-learn, the krippendorff Python package
Language model access through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint
a locally served open-weight model, any commercial API

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check: compute and interpret kappa (20%)
  • Graded labs (40%)
  • Capstone and review (40%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the capstone

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and first-pass human labels, so agreement is measured cleanly. AI-tutor in other labs. AI-full in the capstone.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
