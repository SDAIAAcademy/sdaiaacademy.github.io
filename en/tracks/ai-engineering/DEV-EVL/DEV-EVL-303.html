DEV-EVL-303 Evaluating Retrieval and Grounded Generation

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

AI Engineering

Stream

DEV-EVL Evaluation and Quality Engineering for AI Systems

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: one and a half days with labs and a short evaluation project, with guided pre-work

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • DEV-EVL-301 Evaluation-Driven Development for Language Model Applications
  • Has built or operated a retrieval-augmented application, for example in DEV-LLM-203

Description

Participants evaluate a retrieval-augmented assistant over a synthetic bilingual corpus of public-service regulations. They extend a test set with graded relevance judgements, measure retrieval apart from generation, check that answers are supported, cited and complete, test abstention on unanswerable questions, and attribute failures to parsing, retrieval or generation.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Performance

    Design a retrieval test set covering Arabic and English, dialect and spelling variants, and unanswerable and multi-passage questions.

    Given the corpus, a 40-query seed set and logged queries.

    Evidence 80 or more queries judged on pooled top-five results, with a datasheet of gaps.

  2. Applied

    Compute rank-aware retrieval metrics for two retrievers and compare them with a paired interval.

    Given a lexical and a hybrid retriever.

    Evidence results by language and query type.

  3. Applied

    Implement claim-level support, citation and completeness checks and calibrate the model-graded step against human labels.

    Given 50 labelled answers with a held-out split.

    Evidence evaluator code and a held-out agreement report.

  4. Performance

    Diagnose the main source of failure in a grounded-generation system and decide which component to change first.

    Given the assistant, the suite and a call budget.

    Evidence a one-page diagnosis with a before-and-after run.

Topics

Measuring retrieval

  • Graded relevance and pooling
  • Recall at k, reciprocal rank, discounted gain
  • Evaluating agentic search steps

Building test sets

  • Real, written and filtered synthetic queries
  • Unanswerable, multi-passage and time-sensitive questions
  • Dialect, spelling-variant and Latin-script Arabic queries against Modern Standard Arabic text

Grounding and citations

  • Supported versus correct and complete
  • Citation precision and recall
  • Abstention

Attributing failures

  • Parsing measured on its own
  • Retrieval ceiling
  • Permission leaks

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Ranking-metric library
pytrec_eval, ranx
Grounded-generation evaluators
custom scorers in an evaluation harness, DeepEval, Arize Phoenix evaluators
Arabic text processing
CAMeL Tools, PyArabic

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check: ranking metrics by hand (20%)
  • Graded labs (40%)
  • Suite and memo with defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the suite and memo

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the hand calculation and first relevance judgements. AI-tutor in the labs, where generated queries are filtered and logged. AI-full in the defended project.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
