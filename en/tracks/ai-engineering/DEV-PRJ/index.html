Stream

DEV-PRJ Practicums

Purpose
Give developers evidence of professional performance by shipping and reviewing real AI systems under the eye of experienced engineers.
Who it is for
AI, software and platform engineers who have completed the applied courses of a Developers stream and need supervised, assessed work on real systems.
Suggested order
Take DEV-PRJ-301 (a team practicum for a sponsoring organisation) after the 200 and 300 courses of a Developers stream, then DEV-PRJ-401 (an individual production readiness review) after two of DEV-OPS-401, DEV-SEC-301, DEV-EVL-401 and DEV-INF-404. The data science counterpart is SPC-RSP-302.
