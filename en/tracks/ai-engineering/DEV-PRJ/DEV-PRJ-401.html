DEV-PRJ-401 AI Systems Capstone: Production Readiness Review

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Studio
  • 60 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

AI Engineering

Stream

DEV-PRJ Practicums

Level

Level 400 · Evaluate

Audience

specialist

Format

Studio: about eight weeks part-time, an individual review of an existing AI system with four coached rounds and a panel defence

Total hours

60 h

Prerequisites

  • DEV-PRJ-301, or has shipped and operated an AI system people depend on
  • Two of DEV-OPS-401, DEV-SEC-301, DEV-EVL-401 and DEV-INF-404, or equivalent work
  • Writes architecture decision records and reads latency percentiles

Description

Each participant takes one existing AI system through a production readiness review and decides whether it may go live. The system is a sanitised copy from the participant's organisation, the DEV-PRJ-301 system, or an Academy reference system with seeded weaknesses, run in a sandbox. Participants reconstruct its architecture, load-test and fault-inject it against service objectives, cost it per successful outcome, threat-model and probe it, audit its governance evidence, and defend a go, conditional-go or no-go decision before a panel.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Performance

    Evaluate the architecture against its requirements and reconstruct the key decisions and risks.

    Given design documents and the running system.

    Evidence decision records and the top five risks ranked.

  2. Performance

    Evaluate reliability against service objectives for latency and quality under load and injected faults.

    Given a stated demand envelope.

    Evidence test results against the objectives, with the degradation path observed.

  3. Performance

    Audit cost per successful outcome and identify the largest cost levers.

    Given traces, prices and the measured success rate.

    Evidence a cost model with sensitivity on its two most uncertain inputs.

  4. Performance

    Audit security with a threat model and targeted tests of model, data, tool and supply-chain paths.

    Given a scope agreed with the owner.

    Evidence severity-rated findings with reproduction steps and proposed controls.

  5. Performance

    Review governance evidence for classification, personal data, impact assessment, oversight, incidents and retirement.

    Given a risk management framework.

    Evidence a gap list mapped to the framework, with an owner per gap.

  6. Performance

    Decide go, conditional go or no-go and defend the decision and its conditions before a panel.

    Given the full review.

    Evidence the readiness report, owned conditions and the panel scoring sheet.

Topics

The readiness review

  • Scope, independence and evidence standards
  • Checklists versus judgement
  • Findings with severity and owners

Architecture and reliability

  • Decision records
  • Service objectives for latency and quality
  • Auditing evaluation suites and release gates
  • Load testing to a goodput target and fault injection

Cost and security

  • Token, compute and human review costs
  • Threat models for model, data and agent paths
  • Supply chain, signing and bills of materials

Governance and operations

  • Personal data flows and classification
  • System cards and impact assessment
  • Oversight, incident response and retirement

The decision

  • Go, conditional go and no-go
  • Conditions with owners and dates
  • Briefing decision makers

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Decision records and diagrams
C4 model with Structurizr, Mermaid
Load and fault injection
k6, Locust, Toxiproxy
Observability
OpenTelemetry, Prometheus with Grafana, Langfuse
Security probing and supply chain
garak, PyRIT, OSV-Scanner, CycloneDX
Risk catalogues
OWASP Top 10 lists for LLM and agentic applications, MITRE ATLAS, NIST AI Risk Management Framework

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised check without AI: diagnose an unseen incident from dashboards and a timeline (10%)
  • Coached review rounds (20%)
  • Readiness dossier on a rubric (45%)
  • Panel defence (25%)
To pass
70% overall, a pass from the panel, and at least 60% in each of the five review areas

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and the panel. AI-tutor during review rounds. AI-full for analysis and drafting, disclosed, with every finding reproducible by the participant.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
