DEV-INF-404 AI Solution Architecture and Decision Records

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Intensive
  • 36 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

AI Engineering

Stream

DEV-INF Infrastructure, Performance and Architecture

Level

Level 400 · Evaluate

Audience

specialist

Format

Intensive: 4 teaching days and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

36 h

Prerequisites

  • DEV-SWE-301 System Design for AI Products
  • Has helped deliver an AI or data system to production
  • Data architects may take DEV-DAT-401 in place of DEV-SWE-301

Description

Participants practise the architect's core loop on AI systems: requirements to quality-attribute scenarios, scenarios to options, options to recorded decisions, and decisions to designs others can review and reuse. On fictional public-sector and enterprise cases they write measurable scenarios, draw views as code, compare at least three options on three-year cost, risk and lock-in, keep a decision log, define a reference design with automated fitness checks, and review a peer's architecture alongside data architects.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare quality-attribute scenarios with measurable targets, including regulatory and classification constraints.

    Given a case brief and three stakeholder interviews.

    Evidence a utility tree and a scenario sheet a reviewer can test against.

  2. Performance

    Design an end-to-end architecture with context, container and data-flow views and a per-request latency and cost budget.

    Given the scenario sheet and an enterprise standards list.

    Evidence an architecture document with views as code and one request traced end to end.

  3. Performance

    Evaluate at least three solution options, including a non-generative baseline.

    Given weighted criteria, a three-year volume forecast and price ranges.

    Evidence an options paper with total cost of ownership, risk and lock-in, and a sensitivity analysis.

  4. Performance

    Deliver a decision log whose records state context, options, decision, consequences and review trigger.

    Given the participant's design and a legacy system with no records.

    Evidence at least six records, two of them reconstructed for the legacy system.

  5. Performance

    Review a peer's architecture with a structured trade-off method and recommend approval or a recorded deviation.

    Given a peer design and the reference designs.

    Evidence a review report naming risks, sensitivity points and non-compliance.

  6. Performance

    Design a reference architecture with fitness functions that check conformance automatically.

    Given a recurring pattern such as retrieval over classified documents.

    Evidence a template and two automated checks that fail on a non-conforming design.

Topics

Requirements to scenarios

  • Drivers, constraints and stakeholders
  • AI quality attributes: accuracy, latency, cost, explainability, privacy, oversight
  • Regulation as architecture constraints

Views and patterns

  • Views as code
  • Retrieval, agentic, fine-tuned, classical, batch and event-driven patterns
  • Evaluation, observability, fallback and trust boundaries as components
  • Integration with enterprise systems and data platforms

Options analysis

  • Weighted criteria and their pitfalls
  • Total cost of ownership and sensitivity
  • Lock-in, portability, exit and procurement requirements

Decision records

  • Record structure and review triggers
  • Decision logs as living documents
  • Reconstructing decisions for legacy systems

Reference designs and reviews

  • Reference architectures and fitness functions
  • Structured trade-off reviews and deviations
  • Explaining trade-offs to sponsors

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Architecture views as code
Structurizr DSL, Mermaid, PlantUML
Decision record templates
MADR, a plain Markdown template
Cost and sensitivity modelling
a spreadsheet, a Python notebook
Fitness checks
Open Policy Agent with conftest, tests over infrastructure definitions

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised check without AI assistance: scenarios and one decision record for a short brief (10%)
  • Graded labs (30%)
  • Capstone package on a six-criterion rubric (45%)
  • Panel defence (15%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the capstone rubric

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check. AI-tutor in the labs. AI-full in the capstone, which the participant defends before the panel.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
