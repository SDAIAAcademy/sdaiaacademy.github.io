DEV-INF-405 Regulated and Sovereign Deployment Architecture

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

AI Engineering

Stream

DEV-INF Infrastructure, Performance and Architecture

Level

Level 400 · Evaluate

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: two days with four labs and a defended design

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • Has completed DEV-INF-404, DEV-SEC-401 or DEV-DAT-401, or designs deployment architectures at work
  • Reads a data classification policy and a data-protection impact assessment

Description

Participants turn data rules into deployment architecture for a fictional health and government assistant. They classify data, prompts, embeddings, logs and model weights, trace every flow leaving the approved jurisdiction, place each component, and make the placement verifiable with customer-held keys, attestation, egress denial and signed model import.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Classify a system's data and AI artefacts and analyse every flow crossing a jurisdiction or trust boundary.

    Given a synthetic inventory, vendor documentation and a four-level classification scheme.

    Evidence an asset register and flow inventory with a basis or safeguard per transfer.

  2. Performance

    Design a placement that puts each component in an allowed location.

    Given four placement options with cost and accelerator availability.

    Evidence a placement diagram with decision records traced to the register.

  3. Performance

    Design and test key custody, attestation, egress denial and signed model import.

    Given a lab cluster with a key broker and simulated attestation.

    Evidence a run refusing an unattested workload, a tampered model and an unapproved outbound call.

  4. Performance

    Defend a hybrid deployment design and its evidence pack.

    Given a rule or system change the panel introduces.

    Evidence a scored defence with the placement revised live.

Topics

Rules to requirements

  • Classification and impact assessment
  • Transfer bases, safeguards and risk assessment
  • Hosting rules that defer to data rules

Placement options

  • In-country regions, national providers, on premises, air gap
  • Hosted model APIs: processing region, retention, sub-processors
  • Local open-weight serving; hybrid splits and leakage

Verifiable controls and evidence

  • Customer-held keys, remote attestation and default-deny egress
  • Signed models and bills of materials
  • Evidence packs and approved exceptions

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Key management
OpenBao, HashiCorp Vault
Attestation
Confidential Containers, Intel TDX and AMD SEV tooling
Egress policy
Kubernetes network policies, Cilium
Model signing and bills of materials
OpenSSF model signing, CycloneDX ML-BOM

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check: classify ten artefacts, find three hidden transfers (15%)
  • Graded labs (35%)
  • Capstone design and defence (50%)
To pass
70% overall, no classified artefact outside an allowed location, no transfer without a recorded basis

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check; AI-tutor in labs 1 and 2; AI-full in labs 3 and 4 and the defended capstone.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
