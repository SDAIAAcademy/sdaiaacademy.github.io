Stream

LDS-PRD AI Product and Delivery

Purpose
Prepare product and delivery staff to decide what AI product to build, specify it as testable behaviour, design how people use it, judge it on evidence and deliver it responsibly with engineering teams.
Who it is for
Product managers and owners, service and interaction designers, and delivery leads who own AI features or services, and engineers moving into product roles.
Suggested order
Start with LDS-PRD-201 (discovery and specification), with LDS-PRD-205 for hands-on prototyping; continue to LDS-PRD-202 (evaluation, experiments and economics) and LDS-PRD-203 (interaction design); delivery leads add LDS-PRD-204; LDS-PRD-301 completes the path at launch. Engineers shaping products take DEV-SWE-303; experiment statistics continue in SPC-EXP-301.
