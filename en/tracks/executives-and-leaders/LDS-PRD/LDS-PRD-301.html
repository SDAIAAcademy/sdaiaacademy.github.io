LDS-PRD-301 Responsible AI by Design for Product Teams

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-PRD AI Product and Delivery

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: one and a half days with a simulated launch review

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-PRD-201 AI Product Discovery and Specification
  • Has worked on an AI feature in a pilot or live service
  • Reads evaluation results broken down by user group

Description

Participants take a fictional benefits assistant that drafts eligibility decisions for caseworkers to a launch review: ranked harms, tested safeguards with owners, disclosure and appeal, a measured oversight model, and a go, conditional go or no-go decision. LDS-AIG-303 and LDS-AIG-307 cover impact assessment and oversight procedures in depth.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Analyse harms across affected groups, failure, misuse and scale, ranked by severity, likelihood and reversibility.

    Given the specification, personas and a bias reference list.

    Evidence a ranked harms register that includes non-users.

  2. Applied

    Draft safeguards as requirements with owners, pass or fail tests and subgroup release slices.

    Given the five highest-ranked harms.

    Evidence a safeguard backlog linking each item to a harm and a test.

  3. Applied

    Draft disclosure, explanation and appeal notices for applicants and caseworkers in Arabic and English.

    Given the decision flow.

    Evidence notices on which peers answer four of five comprehension questions correctly.

  4. Performance

    Design a human-oversight model with review sampling, override logs and rubber-stamping measures.

    Given caseworker workload data.

    Evidence an oversight specification with sampling rates and review-trigger thresholds.

  5. Performance

    Decide launch readiness from subgroup results, safeguard tests and open risks.

    Given an evidence pack and a review panel.

    Evidence a launch record with conditions and owners, defended.

Topics

Harms and safeguards

  • Stakeholders who never use the product
  • Failure, misuse, scale and bias harms
  • Safeguards as requirements with tests and owners
  • Subgroup slices in release criteria

Disclosure and contestability

  • Telling people AI is involved
  • Usable explanations and appeal routes

Oversight and launch review

  • Real oversight versus oversight in name only
  • Override logs and automation bias
  • Conditional launches and rollback triggers

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Registers and subgroup results analysis
a notebook with pandas, a spreadsheet pivot table
Safeguard tests
promptfoo, Inspect

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised check on an unseen feature (25%)
  • Graded labs (25%)
  • Launch pack (35%)
  • Panel defence (15%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the check and the pack

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and the first harms list. AI-tutor in labs, set to challenge missing stakeholders. AI-full for notices and the pack, defended unaided.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
