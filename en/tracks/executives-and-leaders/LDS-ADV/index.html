Stream

LDS-ADV Advisory and Value

Purpose
Prepare advisors and data value staff to assess AI opportunities, organisations and data assets on evidence, and to turn each assessment into a costed, lawful recommendation that a sponsor can act on.
Who it is for
Internal and external data and AI consultants, strategy and transformation staff, and data office and finance staff who develop data products or judge AI investments.
Suggested order
Start with LDS-ADV-201 (after one build course) for use-case feasibility, or LDS-ADV-202 for maturity assessment and roadmaps; those who advise other units or clients add LDS-ADV-302; data office and finance staff take LDS-ADV-301. Leaders who own a whole portfolio take LDS-EXE-201 instead.
