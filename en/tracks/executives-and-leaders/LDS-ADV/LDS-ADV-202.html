LDS-ADV-202 Data and AI Maturity Assessment and Roadmaps

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-ADV Advisory and Value

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: two days with labs and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • Names the main data management knowledge areas (LDS-DMG-101 or equivalent)
  • Describes how AI systems learn and fail (USE-FLU-103 or equivalent)

Description

Participants run a data and AI maturity assessment of a fictional water utility: they plan the evidence, interview two role-played managers, review a document pack and a staff survey, and score each dimension from triangulated evidence. They turn gaps into a roadmap by horizon and present it to a sponsor. Compliance audits against national data specifications are LDS-DMG-207.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare an evidence plan of interview guides, a document request and a survey for a chosen maturity model.

    Given extracts from three published models and the utility's organisation chart.

    Evidence a justified model choice and a plan tracing every question to a dimension.

  2. Applied

    Analyse interviews, documents and survey results to score each dimension with a confidence rating.

    Given two interviews, 20 documents and survey data.

    Evidence a scored profile whose evidence references the assessor samples.

  3. Performance

    Design a roadmap that turns gaps into initiatives sequenced by dependency, budget and capacity.

    Given the profile, a sponsor brief and capacity figures.

    Evidence a gap register and a roadmap by horizon with owners and indicators.

  4. Applied

    Prepare and deliver a sponsor readout of findings, roadmap and decisions requested.

    Given 15 minutes with a role-played sponsor.

    Evidence a short deck and a readout rated on a rubric.

Topics

Maturity models

  • Data management knowledge areas and capability models
  • AI adoption dimensions from strategy to impact
  • Maturity is not compliance

Evidence

  • Interviews, documents, surveys and walkthroughs
  • Triangulation, confidence and self-assessment inflation
  • Calibration between assessors

Roadmaps and reporting

  • Targets from strategy, not the top of the scale
  • Data foundations before AI use
  • Readouts that ask for decisions

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Survey tool
LimeSurvey, Microsoft Forms
Spreadsheet and whiteboard for scoring
LibreOffice Calc with Excalidraw, Google Sheets with Miro
Hosted language model for note synthesis
a hosted open-weight model, any approved enterprise assistant

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised scoring of two dimensions, AI-off (25%)
  • Lab artefacts (30%)
  • Roadmap and readout (45%)
To pass
70% overall, with both supervised scores within one level of the reference

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for supervised scoring and interviews. AI-tutor for synthesising notes, each claim citing a note. AI-full for the roadmap and deck, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
