LDS-ADV-302 Advisory Practice for Data and AI Engagements

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-ADV Advisory and Value

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: two days of simulated engagement with role-played clients and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-ADV-202 Data and AI Maturity Assessment and Roadmaps
  • Has written a recommendation for a decision-maker

Description

Participants run a simulated engagement for a fictional department asking whether to build a citizen-facing assistant: they scope the request, facilitate a decision workshop, write an options paper, review an AI-drafted deliverable seeded with errors and resolve conflict-of-interest dilemmas. Feasibility and maturity methods are LDS-ADV-201 and LDS-ADV-202.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Draft an engagement scope with the decision to inform, deliverables, exclusions and acceptance criteria.

    Given an ambiguous request and a role-played discovery call.

    Evidence a one-page scope note and a stakeholder map.

  2. Performance

    Design and facilitate a decision workshop that brings stakeholders with conflicting aims to a recorded decision.

    Given a 20-minute segment with six role-played officials.

    Evidence a workshop plan, the recorded decision and an observer rating.

  3. Performance

    Evaluate three options against stated criteria and recommend one in an options paper that states uncertainty.

    Given an evidence pack.

    Evidence a paper of at most four pages, every claim traced to the pack.

  4. Performance

    Review a deliverable for traceable claims, consistent numbers, real citations and disclosed AI use.

    Given an AI-drafted section seeded with six errors.

    Evidence a review log of defects, severities and corrections.

  5. Applied

    Apply independence, confidentiality and conflict-of-interest principles to advisory dilemmas.

    Given six scenario cards, including a vendor referral fee.

    Evidence a decision per card citing the principle, with disclosure or refusal wording.

Topics

Scoping and stakeholders

  • The decision behind the request
  • Exclusions, assumptions and scope change
  • Stakeholder maps
  • Proposals, work plans and quality checkpoints

Workshops and options papers

  • Diverge, converge, decide
  • Facilitating conflict and quieter voices
  • Evidence versus judgement

Quality and conduct

  • Verifying AI-drafted analysis and citations
  • Disclosing AI use and protecting client data
  • Vendor ties, follow-on work and when to decline

Tools and platforms

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised scope note, AI-off (25%)
  • Observed facilitation (25%)
  • Options paper and readout (35%)
  • Quality review and scenarios (15%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on facilitation

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the scope note and facilitation. AI-tutor for structuring the paper. AI-full for drafting it, disclosed and defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
