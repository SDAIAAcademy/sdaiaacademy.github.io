LDS-DMG-302 Drafting Data and AI Policies and Standards

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-DMG Data Management and Governance Professions

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: 2 days of drafting labs and a mock committee

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-DMG-201 Data Governance Operating Model and Stewardship
  • AI governance staff may take LDS-AIG-201 in its place

Description

Working on a synthetic organisation's policy set, participants turn weak statements into owned, testable requirements traced to their sources, then draft, consult on and defend a generative AI use standard for staff. Rules for data used by AI systems belong to LDS-DMG-301.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Classify statements as policy, standard, procedure or guidance, and record gaps and conflicts.

    Given twelve synthetic governance documents.

    Evidence an annotated document map.

  2. Applied

    Draft testable requirements, each with one obligation, an owner and evidence of compliance.

    Given fifteen vague policy statements.

    Evidence a rewritten set that passes a drafting checklist.

  3. Applied

    Prepare a traceability matrix from external instruments to internal requirements.

    Given excerpts of a privacy law, a classification policy and AI ethics principles.

    Evidence a matrix with no orphan requirements or uncovered obligations.

  4. Performance

    Design an exception and review process with criteria, expiry, compensating controls and review triggers.

    Given three exception requests and a regulatory change.

    Evidence the process and a register of three decided exceptions.

  5. Performance

    Lead the consultation on a generative AI use standard and defend the revised text.

    Given role-played comments from legal, security, business and staff.

    Evidence the standard and comment log, defended before a mock committee.

Topics

Document architecture

  • Policy, standard, procedure, guideline
  • Approval routes
  • Document control: owner, version, review date

Controls language

  • Normative verbs, one obligation per sentence
  • Measures, owners and evidence
  • Words that defeat audit
  • Bilingual drafting with a term base

From source to control

  • Reading instruments for obligations
  • Traceability matrices
  • Machine-readable control catalogues

AI provisions and upkeep

  • Prohibited uses and approved tools by data class
  • Comment dispositions
  • Exceptions that expire; review triggers

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Editor with tracked changes
LibreOffice Writer, any shared document editor
Control catalogue format
OSCAL, a structured spreadsheet
Language model assistant
a hosted open-weight model, any approved commercial assistant

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off supervised rewrite check (25%)
  • Graded drafting labs (35%)
  • Committee review of the capstone (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the first rewrite lab. AI-tutor for tracing. AI-full for drafting the standard, logging a check of each cited source.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
