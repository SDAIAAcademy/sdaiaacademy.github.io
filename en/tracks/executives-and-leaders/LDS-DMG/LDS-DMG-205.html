LDS-DMG-205 Data Sharing, Open Data and Freedom of Information

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-DMG Data Management and Governance Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Workshop: two days with labs, plus a short follow-up task

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-DMG-101 Data Management Foundations
  • Applies classification levels to datasets (LDS-DMG-204 or equivalent)

Description

Participants staff a fictional agency's access desk. They triage sharing requests and draft an agreement, decide a research request, answer information requests with justified redactions, and prepare a dataset for open publication with a re-identification test, metadata and licence. Impact assessment is LDS-DMG-206; anonymisation in depth is DEV-SEC-303.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Apply a sharing test to requests and draft an agreement for an approved one.

    Given ten synthetic requests and a template.

    Evidence a decision log matching the reference on eight, and an agreement passing a clause checklist.

  2. Performance

    Decide a research request for linked records under secondary-use rules.

    Given a request and the agency's data.

    Evidence a memo with conditions, safeguards and access route, defended in review.

  3. Applied

    Prepare a dataset for open publication with a re-identification risk test.

    Given a service dataset with quasi-identifiers.

    Evidence risk before and after treatment, plus a validated metadata record.

  4. Applied

    Draft responses to information requests with recorded grounds for withholding.

    Given five requests and related records.

    Evidence five letters with grounds for any withholding, all within deadline.

  5. Applied

    Measure access performance from response times, usage and reuse.

    Given a request log and download statistics.

    Evidence a report with five indicators.

Topics

Sharing

  • Legitimate purpose, minimisation, single source of truth
  • Routes: service bus, marketplace, secure research environment
  • Agreements and signatories by level
  • Onward use, including training AI models
  • Secondary use for research and public interest

Open data

  • Open by default and grounds to withhold
  • Quasi-identifiers, k-anonymity and its limits, small cells
  • Formats, metadata and licences

Freedom of information

  • The right to information and exemptions
  • Deadlines, partial disclosure, redaction

Performance

  • Response time, approval rate, reuse
  • Publication plans and user feedback

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Re-identification risk and de-identification
ARX, sdcMicro
Open data portal sandbox
CKAN, uData
Logs, agreements and letters
LibreOffice, Microsoft 365

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised case test without AI assistance: six unseen requests (25%)
  • Lab outputs (35%)
  • Performance report and review (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the case test, with no protected record released

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the case test and first triage; AI-tutor for agreements and the research decision; AI-full for letters and the report, with every disclosure decision signed by the participant.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
