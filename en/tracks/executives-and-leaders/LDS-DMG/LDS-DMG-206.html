LDS-DMG-206 Personal Data Protection in Practice

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Intensive
  • 30 h
  • Audience: practitioner

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-DMG Data Management and Governance Professions

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

practitioner

Format

Intensive: 4 teaching days and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

30 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-DMG-101 Data Management Foundations
  • Reads a regulation and extracts who must do what, by when

Description

Participants act as the protection officer of a synthetic telehealth provider and build its case file: records of processing, an impact assessment for a new symptom-checker assistant, answers to rights requests, a transfer assessment and a breach response. Each artefact is reviewed as a regulator would. Privacy-enhancing engineering is DEV-SEC-303; personal data in model training is LDS-AIG-306.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Explain the lawful bases, data subject rights and controller and protection officer duties, and identify which apply to a processing activity.

    Given eight short scenarios.

    Evidence at least seven of eight correct in a supervised check.

  2. Applied

    Prepare records of processing from a data-flow map, with purpose, legal basis, categories, recipients, retention and transfers.

    Given interview notes and system extracts from the case organisation.

    Evidence a flow diagram and a register of ten activities that passes a completeness check.

  3. Applied

    Apply an impact assessment method to an AI-assisted service, rating risks to individuals and selecting proportionate controls.

    Given a symptom-checker assistant that stores conversations.

    Evidence an assessment with risk ratings, controls, residual risk and a recommendation.

  4. Applied

    Analyse data subject requests and draft responses that meet scope, identity, exemption and deadline rules.

    Given six requests, one of them unfounded.

    Evidence a request log and six response letters.

  5. Performance

    Decide whether a cross-border transfer may proceed, and under which safeguard or exemption.

    Given a plan to move support tickets to a cloud region abroad.

    Evidence a transfer risk assessment and decision memo.

  6. Performance

    Lead the response to a personal data breach from containment to notice to the authority and to individuals.

    Given a timed simulation with injected facts.

    Evidence a breach log and both notices, reviewed in a debrief.

Topics

The law and the protection officer

  • Lawful bases, consent and legitimate interest
  • Rights and controller duties
  • Officer appointment triggers, independence, indicators and staff awareness
  • Draft amendments: not in force until gazetted

Records and data flows

  • Flows across systems, processors and countries
  • Records of processing and their upkeep
  • Privacy notices and processor contracts
  • Minimisation, retention and destruction

Impact assessment

  • Triggers: sensitive data, large scale, new technology
  • Pseudonymisation, anonymisation and re-identification tests
  • AI features: conversation logs, training on user data, automated decisions

Rights, transfers and breaches

  • Request intake, identity checks, exemptions and deadlines
  • Adequacy, standard clauses, binding rules and transfer risk assessment
  • Breach assessment, notice content and timing

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised scenario check without AI assistance (20%)
  • Case-file artefacts (40%)
  • Breach simulation and panel defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall, 60% on the check, and no notice missing required content or the deadline

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the first records draft. AI-tutor for reading legal texts. AI-full for letters and notices in the simulation, with every citation verified by the participant.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
