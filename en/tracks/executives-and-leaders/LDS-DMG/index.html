Stream

LDS-DMG Data Management and Governance Professions

Purpose
Prepare the specialists who run an organisation's data management office to produce the registers, rules, decisions and evidence that data and privacy rules require, and to extend them to data used by AI.
Who it is for
Data management, quality, governance, privacy, compliance, classification and access staff, and law, risk and business graduates moving into these roles; no programming is assumed.
Suggested order
Start with LDS-DMG-101, then LDS-DMG-201 for the operating model; take the specialisations LDS-DMG-202 to LDS-DMG-208 according to role card; LDS-DMG-301 and LDS-DMG-302 extend governance to data used by AI and to the drafting of policies and standards.
