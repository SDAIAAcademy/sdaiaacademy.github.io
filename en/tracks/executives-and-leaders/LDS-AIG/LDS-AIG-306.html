LDS-AIG-306 Privacy, Intellectual Property and Content Provenance in AI

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-AIG AI Governance, Risk and Assurance

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: one and a half days with labs and a follow-up review

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-AIG-201 AI Governance Foundations: Principles, Roles and Lifecycle Controls
  • Explains lawful basis and data subject rights (LDS-DMG-206 or equivalent)
  • Runs provided notebook cells

Description

For a fictional publishing group, participants map personal data through a fine-tuning and retrieval pipeline, probe a small fine-tuned model for memorised records, answer a deletion request, decide which training sources may be used, and verify and strip content credentials on generated images. Data access design is LDS-DMG-301; model licences are LDS-AIG-203; privacy-enhancing technologies are DEV-SEC-303.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare a personal data map of a fine-tuning and retrieval pipeline with lawful basis and transfers.

    Given the case documentation.

    Evidence a map that flags every seeded gap.

  2. Applied

    Test a model for disclosure of planted records, with and without an output filter.

    Given a provided small open-weight model fine-tuned on synthetic records containing canaries.

    Evidence disclosure rates logged for both conditions.

  3. Applied

    Analyse a deletion request against weights, index, logs and backups.

    Given the request and the data map.

    Evidence a response stating what can and cannot be removed.

  4. Performance

    Decide for each training source whether to use, exclude or license it, and set output-use rules.

    Given a corpus manifest and three providers' terms.

    Evidence a decision table with reasons.

  5. Performance

    Design a provenance and labelling scheme for generated media and test where it fails.

    Given generated images and common transformations.

    Evidence a report on which signal survives which transformation.

Topics

Personal data in AI

  • Training, retrieval, memory and logs
  • Lawful basis and secondary use
  • Sensitive data for bias testing
  • External model services and transfer

Leakage and rights

  • Memorisation and extraction
  • Output filters
  • Deletion against weights; unlearning claims

Intellectual property

  • Text and data mining exceptions and rights reservations
  • Output ownership and infringement

Provenance

  • Signed content credentials
  • Removable watermarks
  • Labels and deepfake rules

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off scenario check (25%)
  • Graded labs (40%)
  • Review file (35%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check. AI-tutor for reading terms, each clause checked at source. AI-full for the review file, with uncertain legal points marked for counsel.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
