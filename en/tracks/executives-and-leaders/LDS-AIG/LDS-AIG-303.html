LDS-AIG-303 AI Impact Assessment and System Documentation

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-AIG AI Governance, Risk and Assurance

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: two days with labs

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-AIG-201 AI Governance Foundations: Principles, Roles and Lifecycle Controls
  • Reads a model evaluation summary and names what is untested

Description

On a fictional hospital referral-prioritisation system, participants scope an impact assessment, rate impacts on each affected group, write a datasheet, model card, system card and plain-language notice, and assemble an evidence file linking every claim to its source. Risk scoring is LDS-AIG-204; test evidence is LDS-AIG-301; the personal data impact assessment is LDS-DMG-206.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Distinguish impact, risk, personal data and conformity assessments by their triggers.

    Given five described systems.

    Evidence a trigger table correct for four of five.

  2. Applied

    Analyse impacts on each affected group by severity, scale and reversibility, and assign owned mitigations.

    Given case notes and synthetic subgroup results.

    Evidence an impact table that includes the group the notes omit.

  3. Applied

    Draft a datasheet and a model card that state data origin, subgroup results and untested conditions.

    Given synthetic data and an evaluation report.

    Evidence documents in which a peer can trace every claim to a source.

  4. Performance

    Build a system card and a plain-language notice telling affected people how to question a result.

    Given the model card and the clinical review route.

    Evidence a notice on which three peer readers answer comprehension questions correctly.

  5. Performance

    Review a peer's evidence file and decide whether it supports approval at its risk level.

    Given a reviewer checklist.

    Evidence a memo of gaps and a recommendation.

Topics

Assessments and triggers

  • Assessment types and triggers
  • Light and full assessments by risk level
  • The national ethical impact assessment and ISO/IEC 42005

Impact analysis

  • Affected groups, including non-users
  • Rights at stake and impact rating
  • Consulting front-line staff and affected people

Documents by reader

  • Datasheets and model cards
  • System cards
  • Notices and explanations for affected people, in Arabic and English

Evidence files

  • Claim-to-source links
  • Reassessment on change
  • Audit sampling

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Impact assessment template
the national ethical impact assessment, Canada's Algorithmic Impact Assessment
Documentation templates and version control
model and dataset cards on public model hubs, Datasheets for Datasets, Markdown in Git

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check (25%)
  • Graded labs (30%)
  • Capstone and review (45%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the first impact rating. AI-tutor in labs. AI-full for capstone drafting; the participant owns every claim.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
