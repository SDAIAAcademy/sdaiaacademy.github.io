LDS-AIG-304 AI Management Systems and Internal Audit

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Intensive
  • 24 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-AIG AI Governance, Risk and Assurance

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Intensive: 3 teaching days and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up audit

Total hours

24 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-AIG-201 AI Governance Foundations: Principles, Roles and Lifecycle Controls
  • Describes how a plan-do-check-act management system works, for example for quality or information security
  • Reads an AI risk register and its scoring (LDS-AIG-204 or equivalent)

Description

For a fictional logistics company that builds two AI products and buys three AI services, participants set up an AI management system: scope, AI policy, objectives and a statement of applicability. They then plan and run an internal audit with role-played interviews and record sampling, grade findings and plan corrective action. The course builds implementation and internal audit skill; it does not certify.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Explain how the clauses of an AI management system standard call on risk and impact assessment standards.

    Given clause summaries.

    Evidence a one-page map checked against the reference.

  2. Applied

    Prepare the scope, AI policy and measurable objectives of an AI management system.

    Given the case company's profile and obligations.

    Evidence a scope with justified exclusions and owned objectives.

  3. Applied

    Prepare a statement of applicability that justifies each control from the risk assessment.

    Given the reference controls and the company's risk register.

    Evidence a statement giving every control a reason, owner and status.

  4. Performance

    Design a risk-based internal audit programme for one cycle and the plan for one audit.

    Given the management system documents.

    Evidence a programme covering every clause and high-risk control, with auditor independence shown.

  5. Performance

    Audit a function by interview and record sampling, and grade each finding.

    Given role-played interviews and a repository with five seeded nonconformities.

    Evidence a report citing requirement, evidence and grade for each finding, with at least four of the five seeded nonconformities found.

  6. Applied

    Analyse the root cause of a nonconformity and prepare a corrective action with a success measure.

    Given two findings from the participant's audit.

    Evidence corrective action records.

Topics

Management-system logic

  • Plan, do, check, act
  • The clause structure shared with quality and security systems
  • What certification does and does not prove

Scope, policy and controls

  • Roles as developer, provider or user
  • Scope, exclusions and objectives
  • Statement of applicability, linked to LDS-AIG-204 and LDS-AIG-303
  • Controls for bought services, agents and staff-built tools

Internal audit practice

  • Risk-based programmes following management-system audit guidance (ISO 19011)
  • Interviewing, sampling and objective evidence
  • Grading findings
  • Auditor independence

Improvement and external assurance

  • Corrective action and management review
  • Certification bodies and the rules on them
  • The harmonised AI quality management standard
  • National provider accreditation and entity ethics badges

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Control catalogues and crosswalk targets
Cloud Security Alliance AI Controls Matrix, NIST AI Risk Management Framework subcategories, ISO/IEC 42001 reference controls (licensed copy provided)
Compliance register
any spreadsheet, an open-source governance, risk and compliance platform
Evidence repository with audit trail
a Git repository, a document system with version history

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off test: grade six audit observations (20%)
  • Graded labs (30%)
  • Capstone pack (40%)
  • Live defence (10%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the test and on the capstone

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the test and audit interviews. AI-tutor for interpreting clauses. AI-full for capstone prose; findings and grades are the participant's own.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
