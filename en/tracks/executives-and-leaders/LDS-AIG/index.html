Stream

LDS-AIG AI Governance, Risk and Assurance

Purpose
Prepare governance, risk and assurance staff to turn national and international requirements into operating controls, assessed risks and evidence that AI systems stay within approved use.
Who it is for
Governance, risk, compliance, audit, privacy and security staff, and technical staff moving into AI assurance roles in public and private organisations.
Suggested order
Start with LDS-AIG-201, then take LDS-AIG-202, LDS-AIG-203 and LDS-AIG-204 according to role; continue to technical evidence and controls in LDS-AIG-301 and LDS-AIG-302, and to documentation, management systems, incidents, privacy and oversight in LDS-AIG-303 to LDS-AIG-307, before the programme studio LDS-AIG-401.
