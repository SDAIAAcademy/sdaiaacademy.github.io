LDS-AIG-307 Human Oversight and Ethics Review Design

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • Audience: specialist

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-AIG AI Governance, Risk and Assurance

Level

Level 300 · Develop

Audience

specialist

Format

Workshop: one and a half days with labs, a mock review board and a tracked follow-up task

Total hours

12 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-AIG-201 AI Governance Foundations: Principles, Roles and Lifecycle Controls
  • Describes where people intervene when an AI system recommends or acts

Description

Participants act as reviewers in a simulated permit queue where an AI system recommends decisions with seeded errors, and measure what they catch. From that evidence they set oversight models and agent approval thresholds, design appeal routes, and run an ethics review procedure as a mock board. Configuring approval gates in software is LDS-AIG-302; product safeguards are LDS-PRD-301.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Measure the effectiveness of a human review step with seeded errors and review time.

    Given a queue of 60 AI-assisted cases.

    Evidence detection rates and a verdict on whether review is a real control.

  2. Applied

    Configure approval thresholds for an agent's actions by impact and reversibility.

    Given a log of 200 past actions.

    Evidence a replay showing which actions would have been held and the resulting daily approval load.

  3. Performance

    Design a contestability route from notice to human re-decision and redress.

    Given the permit decision flow.

    Evidence a route with time limits and a plain-language notice.

  4. Performance

    Design an ethics review procedure with triggers, independent members, criteria and records.

    Given the case organisation's risk levels.

    Evidence a procedure that decides two cases without amendment.

  5. Performance

    Decide as a board member whether to approve, approve with conditions or reject a proposed AI use.

    Given two dossiers.

    Evidence a decision record defended under challenge.

Topics

Oversight models

  • In the loop, on the loop, in command
  • Authority, competence and time
  • Approval gates and stop rights for agents

Why oversight fails

  • Automation bias and workload
  • Oversight in name only
  • Measuring with seeded errors
  • Override and escalation rates as monitoring signals

Contestability

  • Notice and explanation
  • Appeal and redress

Ethics review

  • Triggers and membership
  • Criteria from national principles
  • Dissent and conditions

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Review queue simulation
a provided web queue, a timed spreadsheet case set
Agent action replay
JupyterLab with a provided replay script, a free hosted notebook
Decision-flow diagrams
Mermaid, draw.io

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check (20%)
  • Graded labs (35%)
  • Capstone and defence (45%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off in the review queue and the check. AI-tutor for testing drafts. AI-full for capstone drafting, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
