LDS-EXE-101 AI for Executives: A Hands-On Briefing

  • Level 100 · Explain
  • Primer
  • 4 h
  • Audience: leadership

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-EXE Executive Leadership

Level

Level 100 · Explain

Audience

leadership

Format

Primer: half a day, hands-on, with a scenario test

Total hours

4 h

Prerequisites

  • None; tracked pre-work: pick and classify one non-confidential task from own work

Description

Executives do one task from their own work with and without an assistant and check it claim by claim, watch an agent attempt two similar multi-step jobs and note where it fails, and leave with questions for any team proposing AI.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Describe where assistants and agents succeed and fail on similar tasks.

    Given the hands-on tasks.

    Evidence an observation sheet per task.

  2. Applied

    Compare assisted and unassisted results on one task, checking facts, figures and sources.

    Given own non-confidential work.

    Evidence claims marked confirmed, wrong or unverifiable.

  3. Applied

    Prepare questions on task, evidence, data, cost, oversight and owner for an AI proposal.

    Given a one-page fictional proposal.

    Evidence eight or more questions, six matching reference criteria.

Topics

What current systems do

  • Assistants, reasoning models, agents
  • Uneven capability; partial credit is not finished work

Reading the claims

  • Benchmark and adoption denominators
  • Self-reported versus measured productivity

Questions for a team

  • Evidence on own cases; cost per completed task
  • Which data may enter which tool; who owns and stops it
  • Use AI only where someone can challenge it

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Hosted assistant
a hosted open-weight model, a commercial assistant
Sandboxed agent
an Academy agent sandbox, a commercial agent mode

How the work is assessed

  • Scenario test, AI-off (70%)
  • Observation sheet and questions (30%)
To pass
Seven of ten on the test; complete question list

Use of AI in the course

AI-full in the hands-on block with non-confidential material; AI-off for the test.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
