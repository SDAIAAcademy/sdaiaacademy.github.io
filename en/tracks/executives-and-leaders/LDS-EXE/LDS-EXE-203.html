LDS-EXE-203 Redesigning Work for Human and Agent Teams

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 16 h
  • Audience: leadership

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-EXE Executive Leadership

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

leadership

Format

Workshop: two days with labs and a tracked follow-up

Total hours

16 h

Prerequisites

  • Has used AI assistants and checked their output (LDS-EXE-101 or equivalent)
  • Manages or advises on a team or process

Description

Participants redesign one process of a fictional organisation for people and agents: they split it into tasks, decide what to automate, augment or keep human, design hand-offs and escalation, and test the design with a hosted agent on synthetic cases. They then plan agent review, three-year role changes and how expertise survives when entry-level tasks move to agents.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Analyse a process into tasks and assign each to a person, an agent or both.

    Given a synthetic process map with volumes and errors.

    Evidence a map stating what each hand-off passes, who checks it and where it escalates.

  2. Applied

    Test an agent on part of the redesign and revise for observed failures.

    Given a hosted agent and twenty synthetic cases.

    Evidence failure counts and the design changes they caused.

  3. Applied

    Draft an agent onboarding and review plan covering scope, permissions, supervision, measures and retirement.

    Given the redesign and an access policy.

    Evidence an agent role card with an owner and review frequency.

  4. Applied

    Prepare a three-year scenario for roles, skills, hiring and redeployment.

    Given a synthetic workforce table.

    Evidence a route for each affected role.

  5. Performance

    Design a plan that protects expertise and the entry-level pipeline through apprenticeship tasks and practice without AI.

    Given the redesign and published deskilling evidence.

    Evidence skills at risk and how each is kept, defended in review.

Topics

Tasks, not jobs

  • Automate, augment or keep human
  • Verifiability, reversibility, judgement
  • Measured versus perceived productivity

Hand-offs and oversight

  • What passes and who accepts it
  • Risk-tiered approvals people read
  • Escalation and stopping

Agents as team members

  • Scope, least privilege, onboarding
  • Review on quality, cost, incidents; a named owner

Workforce and expertise

  • Role scenarios and staff consultation
  • Deskilling and the entry-level pipeline
  • Expertise to challenge AI output

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Process mapping
bpmn.io, draw.io
Sandboxed agent
an Academy agent sandbox, a commercial agent mode

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised task sort, AI-off (20%)
  • Lab artefacts (35%)
  • Redesign pack and review (45%)
To pass
70% overall, at least 60% on the pack

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the task sort; AI-tutor during the redesign; AI-full for the role card and scenario, defended in review.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
