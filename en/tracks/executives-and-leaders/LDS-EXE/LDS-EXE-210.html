LDS-EXE-210 AI Regulation Update: National and Global Briefing

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Primer
  • 4 h
  • Audience: leadership

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-EXE Executive Leadership

Level

Level 200 · Apply

Audience

leadership

Format

Primer: half-day briefing, reissued every six months

Total hours

4 h

Prerequisites

  • Lists the unit's AI systems and the markets it serves

Description

A dated briefing on what changed in AI regulation since the last edition, national instruments first, then the foreign regimes that matter to exporters. Participants verify an AI summary of a new instrument against its text and write a one-page applicability note. Detailed mapping is LDS-AIG-202.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify each change in national and main foreign instruments as binding, advisory or draft.

    Given the change table and four products with their markets.

    Evidence at least 70% on a closed-book scenario test.

  2. Applied

    Analyse an AI summary of a new instrument and verify each claim against the primary text.

    Given one instrument issued since the last edition.

    Evidence claims tagged correct, wrong or unsupported, each with its clause.

  3. Applied

    Prepare an applicability note listing obligations, owners and dates.

    Given the change table and the unit's AI systems.

    Evidence a one-page note passing a five-point checklist.

Topics

National instruments first

  • Data protection enforcement and cybersecurity controls
  • Advisory frameworks and drafts

Foreign regimes for exporters

  • European Union AI Act and its moved dates
  • United States, Chinese and treaty instruments

Acting on change

  • Now, next and watch
  • Owners and review dates

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Document-reading assistant
a hosted open-weight model, any approved enterprise assistant

How the work is assessed

  • Closed-book test (60%)
  • Applicability note (40%)
To pass
70% on the test and a pass on the note

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the test. AI-full in the summary lab, whose point is checking the assistant. AI-tutor for the note.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
