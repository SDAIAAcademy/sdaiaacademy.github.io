LDS-EXE-402 Leading the Enterprise Data Office

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Extended
  • 60 h
  • Audience: leadership

Track

Executives & Leaders

Stream

LDS-EXE Executive Leadership

Level

Level 400 · Evaluate

Audience

leadership

Format

Extended: 10-week part-time cohort with weekly labs, a committee simulation and a panel defence

Total hours

60 h

Prerequisites

  • LDS-DMG-101 Data Management Foundations
  • Has prepared and defended a budget for a function or programme
  • Reads a data governance policy and names the owner, control and evidence for each requirement

Description

A cohort programme for the officer who runs, or will run, an organisation's data office. Week by week, participants write a data office plan: strategy and indicators, governance operating model, maturity and compliance, data readiness for AI, and value. Labs include an AI-readiness review of datasets for a retrieval assistant, a committee simulation on sharing requests and generative AI tool approvals, and a data product value case. Specialist depth is in LDS-DMG-201, LDS-DMG-207 and LDS-DMG-301.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Performance

    Design a data strategy with targets and indicators traced to business objectives and national data indicators.

    Given the entity's corporate strategy and a synthetic baseline.

    Evidence a strategy on a page and an indicator tree with owners.

  2. Performance

    Design the governance operating model: office, committee, owners, stewards, decision rights and escalation.

    Given the entity's structure and national data management controls.

    Evidence an operating-model chart and decision-rights table.

  3. Applied

    Analyse data management maturity and compliance against national specifications.

    Given an evidence pack of documents, metrics and interview notes.

    Evidence a score, evidence reference and gap for each domain.

  4. Performance

    Evaluate whether datasets are ready for training, retrieval or agent access by classification, personal data, quality, lineage and permissions.

    Given three synthetic datasets and a hosted model suggesting classifications.

    Evidence a readiness decision per dataset and a log of overruled model suggestions.

  5. Performance

    Decide data sharing requests and generative AI tool approvals as chair of a data committee.

    Given a simulation with six agenda items.

    Evidence minutes with reasoned decisions, rated by observers.

  6. Applied

    Prepare a value case for a data product with break-even and sensitivity analysis.

    Given synthetic usage and cost data and the national monetisation policy.

    Evidence a value case with two sensitivity cases.

  7. Performance

    Defend a data office plan covering strategy, operating model, compliance, AI readiness, value and budget.

    Given a panel of an executive, a governance specialist and a data engineer.

    Evidence a plan of at most 15 pages and a 30-minute defence.

Topics

Strategy and operating model

  • Indicator trees tied to business and national objectives
  • Office, committee, officers and stewards required by national controls

Maturity and compliance

  • Maturity models and national data index assessments
  • Evidence packs and internal audit
  • Personal data, classification, sharing and open data duties

Data readiness for AI

  • What may be used for training, retrieval and agents
  • Quality, lineage and permission-aware access
  • Approving generative AI tools against privacy and sharing terms

Value and running the function

  • Improve, wrap and sell: three routes to value
  • Budget, team, board reporting and change

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Hosted model for assisted classification
a hosted open-weight model, any approved enterprise assistant
Data catalogue with lineage
OpenMetadata, DataHub
Dashboard tool
Apache Superset, Metabase

How the work is assessed

  • Weekly plan sections (40%)
  • Observed committee simulation (15%)
  • AI-readiness review (10%)
  • Final plan and defence (35%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the defence

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the entry check on governance fundamentals and in the committee simulation. AI-tutor in weekly labs, where the model only suggests classifications the participant confirms or overrules. AI-full for drafting plan sections, defended before the panel.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
