The course catalog for data and artificial intelligence

The catalog describes 301 courses in five tracks, from a first introduction for every employee to advanced work for engineers, data scientists and leaders. Every course page states who the course is for, what it requires, what the participant can do at the end, and how the work is assessed.

The catalog describes the courses; it is not a timetable. Courses are developed and opened in stages. A course whose material is already published carries the mark “Course site”.

Each track is divided into streams. A stream is a group of courses on one subject, with a suggested order.

Two tracks, AI Engineering and Data Science, also hold courses that help prepare for the three levels of the SDAIA professional badge of the same field: Assistant, Associate and Expert. These courses start from six shared foundation courses in the Data & AI Foundations track.

Four levels

The level says what a course asks of the participant. It is not a job grade. The first digit of the number in a course code is its level: AE-302 is a level 300 course.

  1. 100

    Explain

    The participant describes ideas, tells them apart and uses them with guidance. No earlier knowledge of the subject is assumed.

    Courses 35

  2. 200

    Apply

    The participant applies standard methods to defined tasks, with support.

    Courses 105

  3. 300

    Develop

    The participant builds complete, working results independently and defends the choices made.

    Courses 113

  4. 400

    Evaluate

    The participant designs for others, reviews and judges alternatives, and handles new problems.

    Courses 48

How to read a course code

A code has three parts: the track prefix, the stream and a number. DEV-AGT-301 is a course in the AI Engineering track, in the stream Agentic Systems, at level 300. Courses that help prepare for a badge have short codes: FN for the shared foundations, AE for AI Engineering and DS for Data Science.
