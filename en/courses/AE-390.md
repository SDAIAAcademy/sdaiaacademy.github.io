# AE-390 · AI Engineering Practicum

- Track: AI Engineering
- Path: [AI Engineering: courses that help prepare for the Associate level](../paths/AE-300.md)
- Level: Level 300 · Develop
- Format: Practicum: about eight weeks part-time in teams of three to five for a sponsoring organisation, with weekly reviews by experienced engineers, two gate reviews and a defence before the sponsor
- Total hours: 100 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Teams of three to five take one problem from a sponsoring government entity or company from scoping to a shipped AI system in about eight weeks part-time. They agree a specification and acceptance criteria with the sponsor, build in weekly iterations reviewed by experienced engineers, evaluate against the criteria with a regression gate, harden the system after another team attacks it, deploy it with service objectives, monitoring and a rehearsed rollback, and hand it over with an evaluation report, a security note and an operations plan. Sponsor data enters only as an anonymised extract or a synthetic replica built to the sponsor's schema.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse a sponsor's problem through discovery and translate it into a specification with acceptance criteria and a data-handling plan.
- Build, as a team, a working AI system that meets the agreed acceptance criteria, with coding-agent output reviewed in proportion to risk.
- Evaluate the system against the sponsor's criteria with error analysis and a regression gate.
- Harden the system after a cross-team attack and record the residual risks the sponsor must accept.
- Deploy the system with service objectives, capacity sizing, monitoring, cost per successful task and a rehearsed rollback.
- Defend the system, the team's design decisions and one's own contribution before the sponsor and experienced engineers.

## Topics

### Engagement and scoping

- Sponsor roles, agreements and intellectual property
- Discovery and the problem brief
- Acceptance criteria as the contract
- Change control during delivery

### Team delivery

- Weekly iterations and demonstrations
- Coding agents with review by risk
- Tests, continuous integration and decision records

### Data and governance

- Classification of sponsor data
- Anonymised extracts and synthetic replicas
- Data sent to external models
- Impact assessment and system card

### Quality and security

- Owning the evaluation set
- Error analysis and regression gates
- Threat model and cross-team attack day

### Operations and hand-over

- Service objectives, capacity and monitoring
- Cost per successful task
- Runbooks, rollback drills and ownership after the practicum
