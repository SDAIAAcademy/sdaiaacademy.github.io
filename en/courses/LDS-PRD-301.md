# LDS-PRD-301 · Responsible AI by Design for Product Teams

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [AI Product and Delivery](../paths/LDS-PRD.md)
- Level: Level 300 · Develop
- Format: Workshop: one and a half days with a simulated launch review
- Total hours: 12 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Participants take a fictional benefits assistant that drafts eligibility decisions for caseworkers to a launch review: ranked harms, tested safeguards with owners, disclosure and appeal, a measured oversight model, and a go, conditional go or no-go decision. LDS-AIG-303 and LDS-AIG-307 cover impact assessment and oversight procedures in depth.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse harms across affected groups, failure, misuse and scale, ranked by severity, likelihood and reversibility.
- Draft safeguards as requirements with owners, pass or fail tests and subgroup release slices.
- Draft disclosure, explanation and appeal notices for applicants and caseworkers in Arabic and English.
- Design a human-oversight model with review sampling, override logs and rubber-stamping measures.
- Decide launch readiness from subgroup results, safeguard tests and open risks.

## Prerequisites

- [LDS-PRD-201 · AI Product Discovery and Specification](LDS-PRD-201.md)
- Has worked on an AI feature in a pilot or live service
- Reads evaluation results broken down by user group

## Topics

### Harms and safeguards

- Stakeholders who never use the product
- Failure, misuse, scale and bias harms
- Safeguards as requirements with tests and owners
- Subgroup slices in release criteria

### Disclosure and contestability

- Telling people AI is involved
- Usable explanations and appeal routes

### Oversight and launch review

- Real oversight versus oversight in name only
- Override logs and automation bias
- Conditional launches and rollback triggers
