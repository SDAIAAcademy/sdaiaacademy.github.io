# USE-SEC-206 · AI in Financial Services

- Track: Data & AI Fluency
- Path: [AI in Sectors](../paths/USE-SEC.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Workshop: two days of labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map
- Total hours: 16 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Managers who own risk in banking, payments and insurance work on a fictional digital bank with synthetic and public data. They set fraud and anti-money-laundering alert thresholds, compare a credit model with a scorecard, check decline reasons and test a service assistant for binding commitments, ending with a defended map. Individual finance work is USE-PRO-203.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Explain the model risk lifecycle, from inventory to monitoring, and who holds each duty.
- Compute what fraud and laundering models catch, miss and cost in review work at three thresholds.
- Analyse a machine-learning credit model against a scorecard for accuracy, stability, group approval rates and decline reasons.
- Test a customer service assistant for wrong commitments, unsupported advice and data disclosure.
- Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking financial use cases by value, data readiness, model risk tier and explanation duty.

## Prerequisites

- [USE-FLU-103 · How AI Systems Learn and Where They Fail](USE-FLU-103.md)
