## Description

Participants redesign one process of a fictional organisation for people and agents: they split it into tasks, decide what to automate, augment or keep human, design hand-offs and escalation, and test the design with a hosted agent on synthetic cases. They then plan agent review, three-year role changes and how expertise survives when entry-level tasks move to agents.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse a process into tasks and assign each to a person, an agent or both.
- Test an agent on part of the redesign and revise for observed failures.
- Draft an agent onboarding and review plan covering scope, permissions, supervision, measures and retirement.
- Prepare a three-year scenario for roles, skills, hiring and redeployment.
- Design a plan that protects expertise and the entry-level pipeline through apprenticeship tasks and practice without AI.

## Topics

### Tasks, not jobs

- Automate, augment or keep human
- Verifiability, reversibility, judgement
- Measured versus perceived productivity

### Hand-offs and oversight

- What passes and who accepts it
- Risk-tiered approvals people read
- Escalation and stopping

### Agents as team members

- Scope, least privilege, onboarding
- Review on quality, cost, incidents; a named owner

### Workforce and expertise

- Role scenarios and staff consultation
- Deskilling and the entry-level pipeline
- Expertise to challenge AI output
