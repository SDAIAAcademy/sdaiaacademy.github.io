# LDS-EXE-209 · AI Incident Simulation for Leadership Teams

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [Executive Leadership](../paths/LDS-EXE.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Simulation: one facilitated day with a briefing, a four-phase tabletop with timed injects and a structured debrief
- Total hours: 6 h
- Audience: leadership
- Status: In development

## Description

Leadership teams rehearse an AI incident before they face one. In a sandbox, participants first read the traces of a fictional public-service entity's assistant and procurement agent to see how the facts of a failure are found. They then run a timed four-phase tabletop: a harmful Arabic answer spreads on social media; a prompt injection in a supplier email makes the agent send personal data outside the entity; the data protection authority writes; the press and the board demand answers. Teams keep a decision log and each participant writes an after-action memo. Board escalation basics are in LDS-EXE-206; operational incident handling is LDS-AIG-305.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Describe incident roles, decision rights and escalation thresholds, from technical lead to accountable executive.
- Use agent traces and logs to establish what happened, what data moved and what is still unknown.
- Classify each incident by severity and identify the notifications it triggers under data protection law, sector rules and contracts.
- Decide containment, disclosure and recovery actions under time pressure, such as pausing the agent or rolling back a model version.
- Prepare an after-action memo with control changes, owners and dates.

## Prerequisites

- Reads a one-page incident summary and a likelihood and impact matrix
- Has read the sample incident procedure sent as pre-work

## Topics

### How AI incidents differ

- Harmful or false output at scale
- Agents that act: tool misuse after prompt injection
- Evidence in prompts, traces and tool calls
- Vendor models and shared responsibility

### Roles and thresholds

- Incident roles and decision rights
- Severity matrix and escalation
- Using the AI inventory and risk register at the start

### Decisions under pressure

- Containment options and their costs: pause, roll back, switch off a tool
- Preserving evidence
- Notifying with incomplete facts
- Holding statements for the public, staff, regulator and board

### After the exercise

- After-action review
- Control, inventory and risk-register changes
- Re-testing before restart
