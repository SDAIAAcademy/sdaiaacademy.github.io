# LDS-EXE-301 · AI in Public Policy and Public Service Delivery

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [Executive Leadership](../paths/LDS-EXE.md)
- Level: Level 300 · Develop
- Format: Intensive: 3 teaching days and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task
- Total hours: 25 h
- Audience: leadership
- Status: In development

## Description

Senior public servants practise choosing the instruments a government uses to shape AI and making the case for AI in a public service on public-value terms. Participants appraise options for one policy problem, from guidance and standards to procurement, funding, sandboxes and shared compute; build a public-value case for a citizen-facing assistant; test it on a fixed Arabic and English case set with hosted models; and assemble the accountability pack the public is owed. Sector use cases are in USE-SEC-203 and contract terms in LDS-EXE-205.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse regulation, guidance, standards, procurement, funding, sandboxes, data sharing and shared compute as policy instruments by reach, cost and speed.
- Analyse the public value, costs, risks and effects by user group of an AI-enabled public service.
- Test a citizen-facing assistant for accuracy, refusals, escalation and equal treatment across languages.
- Design the accountability arrangements for a public AI system: inventory entry, transparency record, impact assessment, human review and appeal.
- Evaluate a ministry's AI competence needs and set a role-based development plan.
- Defend a policy brief and public-value case before a mock ministerial committee.

## Prerequisites

- [LDS-EXE-201 · AI Strategy and Use-Case Portfolio](LDS-EXE-201.md)
- Has drafted or reviewed a policy paper or public-service business case
- Reads a cost-benefit table

## Topics

### The state's roles in AI

- Regulator, funder, buyer, operator and data steward
- Instruments and their trade-offs: sandboxes, standards, shared compute
- Life-cycle cost, local content and independent testing in public procurement

### Public value

- Public value versus private return
- Cost-benefit with distributional effects
- Service outcomes, not adoption
- When a public decision should not be automated

### Accountability to the public

- Use-case inventories and transparency records
- Impact assessment and independent review
- Human review, contestability and redress
- Equal service in Arabic and English

### Civil-service capability

- Accountable AI officers in government
- Role-based competence and training duties
- Evaluating capability programmes
