LDS-DMG-207

Compliance Evidence and Data Maturity Assessment

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Intensive
  • 4 days · 24 h
  • Available

Participants act as the compliance specialist of a synthetic public entity preparing for its annual national data assessment. They judge a repository of realistic evidence (policies, minutes, screenshots, logs), score maturity, compute operational metrics, and measure how often an AI assistant maps documents to the wrong specification. The result is an audit-ready evidence pack, a risk-ranked findings register and a corrective plan, tested in a mock audit. Roadmaps for sponsors belong to LDS-ADV-202.

Who it is for

Audience: practitioner

Data management, quality, governance, privacy, compliance, classification and access staff, and law, risk and business graduates moving into these roles; no programming is assumed.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Describe how a national data assessment scores compliance, maturity and operational excellence, and what evidence each part needs.

  2. Applied

    Prepare acceptance criteria stating what must exist, who approved it and what proves it operates.

  3. Applied

    Classify evidence as sufficient, partial or insufficient, recording the reason and the gap.

  4. Applied

    Compute maturity scores and operational metrics, tracing every level claimed to its evidence.

  5. Performance

    Evaluate an AI-assisted mapping of documents to specifications and decide where the compliance process may use it.

  6. Performance

    Deliver an evidence pack with a risk-ranked findings register and a corrective plan with owners and dates.

More about this course

Topics

Assessment methods

  • Inventory of applicable instruments and obligations
  • Binary scoring of specifications and priorities
  • Maturity questionnaires and level descriptors
  • Operational metrics from national platforms
  • Comparison with DAMA-DMBOK and DCAM maturity models

Evidence

  • Existence, approval, implementation, effectiveness
  • Sufficiency, currency and scope
  • Sampling and walk-throughs

Findings and the audit cycle

  • Findings, root causes and risk ranking
  • Corrective plans and verified closure
  • Readiness calendar and reporting to the data management committee

AI in compliance work

  • Assisted evidence mapping
  • Measuring assistant errors against a key
  • Human sign-off and audit trail

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Compliance and findings register
LibreOffice Calc with course templates, Redmine or another issue tracker
Versioned document repository
Nextcloud, Git with an evidence index
Metrics dashboard
Apache Superset, Metabase
Assistant over a document set
a hosted open-weight model, any approved commercial assistant

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised check without AI assistance: judge twelve unseen evidence items (25%)
  • Graded lab artefacts (35%)
  • Mock audit (40%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 70% agreement with the key on the supervised check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check and the first sufficiency lab. AI-tutor for questionnaire scoring. AI-full in the mapping lab and the capstone, where the participant defends every judgement.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
