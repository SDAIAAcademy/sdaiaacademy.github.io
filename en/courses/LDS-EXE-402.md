# LDS-EXE-402 · Leading the Enterprise Data Office

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [Executive Leadership](../paths/LDS-EXE.md)
- Level: Level 400 · Evaluate
- Format: Extended: 10-week part-time cohort with weekly labs, a committee simulation and a panel defence
- Total hours: 60 h
- Audience: leadership
- Status: In development

## Description

A cohort programme for the officer who runs, or will run, an organisation's data office. Week by week, participants write a data office plan: strategy and indicators, governance operating model, maturity and compliance, data readiness for AI, and value. Labs include an AI-readiness review of datasets for a retrieval assistant, a committee simulation on sharing requests and generative AI tool approvals, and a data product value case. Specialist depth is in LDS-DMG-201, LDS-DMG-207 and LDS-DMG-301.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Design a data strategy with targets and indicators traced to business objectives and national data indicators.
- Design the governance operating model: office, committee, owners, stewards, decision rights and escalation.
- Analyse data management maturity and compliance against national specifications.
- Evaluate whether datasets are ready for training, retrieval or agent access by classification, personal data, quality, lineage and permissions.
- Decide data sharing requests and generative AI tool approvals as chair of a data committee.
- Prepare a value case for a data product with break-even and sensitivity analysis.
- Defend a data office plan covering strategy, operating model, compliance, AI readiness, value and budget.

## Prerequisites

- [LDS-DMG-101 · Data Management Foundations](LDS-DMG-101.md)
- Has prepared and defended a budget for a function or programme
- Reads a data governance policy and names the owner, control and evidence for each requirement

## Topics

### Strategy and operating model

- Indicator trees tied to business and national objectives
- Office, committee, officers and stewards required by national controls

### Maturity and compliance

- Maturity models and national data index assessments
- Evidence packs and internal audit
- Personal data, classification, sharing and open data duties

### Data readiness for AI

- What may be used for training, retrieval and agents
- Quality, lineage and permission-aware access
- Approving generative AI tools against privacy and sharing terms

### Value and running the function

- Improve, wrap and sell: three routes to value
- Budget, team, board reporting and change
