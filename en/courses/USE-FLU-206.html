USE-FLU-206

Creating Images, Audio and Video with AI Responsibly

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 8 h
  • In development

Participants produce images, voice-overs and short videos for work and publish them responsibly. They brief and refine assets, check text, Arabic lettering, symbols and cultural detail, decide when consent or rights clearance is needed, refuse impersonation, apply labels and provenance metadata, inspect received media, and add captions, alternative text and transcripts. A tracked follow-up applies the method to one real communication need.

Who it is for

Audience: practitioner

Staff in any role and sector, from new hires to managers, who use or will use dashboards, AI assistants and agents in routine work; no technical background is assumed and delivery is Arabic-first in the Kingdom.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare a media brief and use generation tools to produce matching assets, logging prompts and settings.

  2. Applied

    Test generated assets for factual, textual, cultural and visual errors, including Arabic lettering.

  3. Applied

    Classify a media use by consent, rights and impersonation risk and decide whether it may proceed.

  4. Applied

    Apply labels, provenance metadata, captions, alternative text and transcripts to finished assets.

  5. Awareness

    Explain what provenance metadata and watermarks can and cannot prove about a received file.

Format and days

More about this course

Topics

Briefing and checking

  • Purpose, audience and message first
  • Text, hands, symbols and Arabic script
  • Cultural and religious sensitivities

Rights and consent

  • Likeness and voice of real people
  • Photos of identifiable people as personal data
  • Tool licences and copyright

Labelling and provenance

  • Visible labels and content credentials
  • Removable watermarks
  • Checking received media

Accessible media

  • Alternative text
  • Captions and transcripts

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Image generation
an open-weight model in ComfyUI on a free notebook GPU, an approved commercial generator
Voice and captions
an open-weight text-to-speech model, Whisper for draft captions
Provenance inspection
a Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) inspector, reverse image search

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off check on six assets (30%)
  • Lab artefacts (40%)
  • Follow-up asset and log (30%)
To pass
70% overall and no unlabelled synthetic person, voice or scene

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the check, quality hunt and consent decisions; AI-tutor for the brief; AI-full in generation and the follow-up, where the participant owns each asset.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
