## Description

Participants run a data and AI maturity assessment of a fictional water utility: they plan the evidence, interview two role-played managers, review a document pack and a staff survey, and score each dimension from triangulated evidence. They turn gaps into a roadmap by horizon and present it to a sponsor. Compliance audits against national data specifications are LDS-DMG-207.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Prepare an evidence plan of interview guides, a document request and a survey for a chosen maturity model.
- Analyse interviews, documents and survey results to score each dimension with a confidence rating.
- Design a roadmap that turns gaps into initiatives sequenced by dependency, budget and capacity.
- Prepare and deliver a sponsor readout of findings, roadmap and decisions requested.

## Prerequisites

- Names the main data management knowledge areas (LDS-DMG-101 or equivalent)
- Describes how AI systems learn and fail (USE-FLU-103 or equivalent)

## Topics

### Maturity models

- Data management knowledge areas and capability models
- AI adoption dimensions from strategy to impact
- Maturity is not compliance

### Evidence

- Interviews, documents, surveys and walkthroughs
- Triangulation, confidence and self-assessment inflation
- Calibration between assessors

### Roadmaps and reporting

- Targets from strategy, not the top of the scale
- Data foundations before AI use
- Readouts that ask for decisions
