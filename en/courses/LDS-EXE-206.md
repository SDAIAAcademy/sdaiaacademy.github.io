# LDS-EXE-206 · Governing AI in the Organisation: Boards and Executives

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [Executive Leadership](../paths/LDS-EXE.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Workshop: two days with labs, a tabletop and a tracked follow-up
- Total hours: 16 h
- Audience: leadership
- Status: In development

## Description

Participants set up board oversight of AI for a fictional organisation: risk appetite, inventory review, impact assessment triggers, and agent and vendor expectations, tested by running an agent under two permission settings and in a board tabletop. Operating controls are in LDS-AIG-201; full incident exercises in LDS-EXE-209.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Draft a risk appetite statement with tolerances by risk level and decisions reserved for the board.
- Analyse an AI inventory for missing systems, owners, risk levels and review needs.
- Specify board expectations for agent controls and vendor assurance, from permissions and stopping to certificate scope.
- Decide escalation and disclosure in a vendor-model incident.

## Prerequisites

- Sits on, reports to or supports a board or executive committee
- Has used and checked AI assistants (LDS-EXE-101 or equivalent)

## Topics

### Accountability and appetite

- Board, executive, owner and assurance roles
- Reserved decisions; three lines of defence
- Tolerances by risk level

### Inventory, agents and assurance

- The inventory as the board's view
- Triggers for impact assessment and review
- Least privilege, approvals, logging, stopping
- Certificate scope, ethics badges, provider accreditation

### Oversight in practice

- Board reporting on measures and incidents
- Escalation and disclosure
- National rules first, foreign regimes for exporters
