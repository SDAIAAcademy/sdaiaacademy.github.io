# DEV-DAT-401 · Data Architecture

- Track: AI Engineering
- Path: [Data Engineering and Architecture](../paths/DEV-DAT.md)
- Level: Level 400 · Evaluate
- Format: Intensive: 4 teaching days and a review-and-defence day, with guided pre-reading and a tracked follow-up task
- Total hours: 36 h
- Audience: specialist
- Status: In development

## Description

Participants act as data architect for a synthetic group of public service entities moving from a central warehouse and scattered lakes to a governed platform. They turn interviews into quality-attribute scenarios, compare a central lakehouse, federated data products and a hybrid on five-year cost, design the catalogue as the control point across engines, place workloads by classification, set standards for federated teams, review a peer design and a vendor proposal, and defend a roadmap. Solution-architecture practice is shared with DEV-INF-404.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse business, regulatory and operational requirements into quality-attribute scenarios.
- Design a target architecture covering storage, catalogue, engines, integration, semantic layer and serving to analytics and AI.
- Evaluate three architecture options on five-year cost of ownership and risk.
- Design standards for federated ownership: data product descriptors, contracts, naming and the supported platform path.
- Review a design and a vendor proposal against the standards and decide on deviations.
- Design a migration roadmap with transition states, legacy retirement and measurable milestones.

## Prerequisites

- [DEV-DAT-201 · Modern Data Engineering](DEV-DAT-201.md)
- [DEV-DAT-301 · Data Modelling: Conceptual to Semantic](DEV-DAT-301.md)
- Has designed and operated a production pipeline or platform component

## Topics

### Foundations

- Replication, partitioning, transactions and consistency
- Batch, streaming and serving paths
- Integration: event-driven, API-based and extract-load-transform
- Quality attributes and trade-offs

### Platform shapes

- Warehouse, lakehouse on open table formats, multi-engine designs
- The catalogue as control point for names, credentials and policy
- Semantic layers serving reports, APIs and agents

### Federated ownership

- Data products, domain teams and the platform team
- Contracts and interoperability
- When federation pays; data fabric claims tested

### Placement and cost

- Classification-driven placement and residency; sovereign deployment is DEV-INF-405
- Security zones, encryption and access architecture
- Cost of ownership and unit cost
- Lock-in, open formats and exit plans

### Architecture practice

- Decision records and reference architectures
- Reviews and deviation approval
- Roadmaps and retirement
- Reviewing AI-generated designs
