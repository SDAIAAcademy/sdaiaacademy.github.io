LDS-AIG-401

Designing and Running an AI Governance Programme

  • Level 400 · Evaluate
  • Studio
  • 40 h
  • In development

Each participant designs, and rehearses running, an AI governance programme for their own organisation or a fictional ministry with 60 AI systems, agents in pilot and many staff-built tools. Week by week they diagnose the current state, set the mandate and operating model, build a tiered policy set and review gates with service levels, write committee terms of reference and define metrics, resourcing and training. They chair a simulated committee and defend the programme before a panel. Policy drafting technique is LDS-DMG-302; the wider AI office is LDS-EXE-401.

Who it is for

Audience: leadership

Governance, risk, compliance, audit, privacy and security staff, and technical staff moving into AI assurance roles in public and private organisations.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Analyse an organisation's AI governance against its obligations and a maturity model.

  2. Performance

    Design the mandate, operating model and decision rights, choosing central, federated or hub-and-spoke.

  3. Performance

    Design a tiered policy set and proportionate review gates with service levels and exceptions.

  4. Performance

    Lead a governance committee to decisions on contested cases, recording reasons and dissent.

  5. Performance

    Design programme metrics, board reporting and a resourcing plan.

  6. Performance

    Defend the programme and its 18-month roadmap before a panel and revise it under challenge.

Format and days

Format
Studio: eight weeks part-time, coached design with three milestone reviews, a committee simulation and a panel defence
Total hours
40 h

More about this course

Topics

Diagnosis and mandate

  • Maturity models and evidence
  • Obligations and risk appetite
  • Charter and links to risk, privacy, security and audit

Operating models

  • Central, federated, hub-and-spoke
  • Committee, office, system owners and champions
  • Governance as a service

Policies and gates

  • Policy, standard and procedure tiers
  • Rules for agents and staff-built tools
  • Gates with service levels and exceptions

Committees

  • Terms of reference and quorum
  • Decisions, dissent and conflicts of interest
  • Escalation to the board

Measuring and sustaining

  • Effectiveness over activity counts
  • Capacity and budget
  • Training by role and annual review
  • Internal audit, certification and entity ethics badges

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Versioned programme documents
Markdown in Git, an editor with tracked changes
Governance register
any spreadsheet, an open-source governance, risk and compliance platform
Metrics and reporting
a notebook with plotting libraries, an open-source dashboard such as Apache Superset or Metabase

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised AI-off case analysis of an unseen governance failure (15%)
  • Three milestone reviews (25%)
  • Committee simulation (15%)
  • Dossier and panel defence (45%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the case analysis and on the panel defence

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the case analysis and the committee simulation. AI-tutor in milestone preparation, set to challenge. AI-full for drafting the dossier, defended element by element.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
