DS-206

Analysis with AI Assistants: Delegate, Verify, Reproduce

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Intensive
  • 4 days · 21 h
  • Available

On the learning path: Data Science

Participants analyse data with a coding assistant or agent in a notebook and stay accountable for every number. For a fictional courier company they decide which steps to delegate, brief the assistant with acceptance checks, prove results by recomputing key numbers another way, measure how answers vary between runs, decide which data may go to which assistant, and package an analysis that a peer reruns from logged prompts, code and data.

Who it is for

Audience: practitioner

Graduates and early-career analysts who have completed the FN courses or passed the placement test, and analysts moving into data science.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify which analysis steps suit delegation and which need the analyst's own judgement, with a reason for each.

  2. Applied

    Prepare a delegation brief stating question, data, definitions, constraints and acceptance checks before the assistant runs.

  3. Applied

    Test assisted analyses by recomputing headline numbers another way and checking joins, filters, denominators and chart encodings.

  4. Applied

    Measure run-to-run variability by repeating one request five times and comparing code, numbers and conclusions.

  5. Applied

    Classify analysis data by sensitivity and assign each table to a local assistant, a hosted assistant or neither.

  6. Performance

    Deliver an assisted analysis package that another analyst can rerun and audit from logged prompts, code, data and verification notes.

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised check, AI-off: find the errors in a printed assisted analysis and recompute two numbers in SQL (20%)
  • Capstone package scored on a rubric: brief and delegation choices 15, verification notes 30, variability and reproducibility 25, data handling and disclosure 15, findings 15 (60%)
  • Live defence without the assistant (20%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on both the capstone rubric and the supervised check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and the first recomputation in each verification lab. AI-full in the delegation labs and the capstone, because working with assistants is the subject, and the participant defends every number without one. AI-tutor for the pre-work.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
