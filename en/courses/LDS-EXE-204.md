## Description

Participants design an AI adoption programme for a fictional organisation whose staff use AI unevenly and mostly without guidance. From synthetic usage, survey and help-desk data they find shallow, unsafe or blocked use, then write role-based literacy outcomes, a manager kit, incentives that reward value rather than activity, a champions plan, an adoption dashboard and a 90-day plan.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse adoption data to find shallow, unsafe or blocked use and its barrier.
- Draft role-based literacy outcomes, each with a check, for what staff must do with AI.
- Prepare a manager support kit and incentives that reward measured value and safe use.
- Configure a dashboard reporting use, skill and value separately, each defined.
- Design a 90-day adoption plan with champions, a community of practice, measures and decision points.

## Prerequisites

- Has used AI assistants on own work (LDS-EXE-101 or equivalent)
- Leads a unit, a function or a change programme

## Topics

### Why adoption stalls

- Use is not value
- Unguided and unsanctioned use
- Managers and culture decide

### Literacy by role

- The all-professionals baseline
- Outcomes for staff, managers, specialists
- Checking skill with and without AI

### Managers, rules and incentives

- Manager time, permission and examples
- Data rules people can follow
- Rewarding value and safe use

### Champions and measurement

- Champion networks (training champions is USE-FLU-303)
- Baselines and comparison groups
- Scale, fix or stop
