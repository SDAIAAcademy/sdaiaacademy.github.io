LDS-AIG-305

AI Incident Response and Compliance Monitoring

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Workshop
  • 12 h
  • In development

On a fictional insurer's claims assistant and document agent, participants triage incident reports, reconstruct an agent data leak from traces, decide who must be notified, lead a blameless review and test controls by sampling. Trace logging design is LDS-AIG-302; leadership rehearsal is LDS-EXE-209; reliability incidents are DEV-OPS-401.

Who it is for

Audience: specialist

Governance, risk, compliance, audit, privacy and security staff, and technical staff moving into AI assurance roles in public and private organisations.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Classify incident reports by type, harm, severity and failed component.

  2. Applied

    Analyse traces and change records to reconstruct an incident and the controls that failed.

  3. Applied

    Draft the notices a breach requires to the data protection regulator, affected people and a supplier.

  4. Performance

    Lead a blameless post-incident review and decide corrective actions.

  5. Performance

    Design a compliance monitoring plan of control tests, thresholds and escalation.

More about this course

Topics

Incidents and triage

  • Incidents, near misses, breaches and non-compliance
  • AI-specific types: harmful output, unfair outcome, undisclosed AI use, agent overreach
  • Reporting frameworks and incident databases
  • Containment: rollback, switch-off, human-only mode

Investigation and notification

  • Evidence preservation and chain of custody
  • Reconstruction from traces
  • Breach duties and deadlines
  • Telling affected people and suppliers

Learning and monitoring

  • Blameless review
  • Incidents as test cases
  • Control testing by sampling
  • Reporting to the committee

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Public incident records
AI Incident Database, OECD AI Incidents Monitor
Case tracking
an open-source issue tracker, any ticketing tool

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised, timed AI-off triage test (25%)
  • Graded labs (35%)
  • Capstone exercise (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the triage test

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the triage test and first triage in the exercise. AI-tutor for reading traces. AI-full for the playbook, which the participant defends.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
