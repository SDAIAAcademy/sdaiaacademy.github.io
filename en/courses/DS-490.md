# DS-490 · Data Science Capstone: Defended Analysis

- Track: Data Science
- Path: [Data Science: courses that help prepare for the Expert level](../paths/DS-400.md)
- Level: Level 400 · Evaluate
- Format: Studio: coached individual project over about eight weeks part-time, with milestone reviews, a peer review and a panel defence
- Total hours: 60 h
- Audience: specialist
- Status: In development

## Description

Each participant takes one decision question from the case bank of the fictional Al-Arid Regional Development Authority (school transport, clinic opening hours, a small-business grant scheme, ambulance standby locations), or an approved, anonymised question from their employer, and carries it from framing to a defended recommendation. The analysis has a design fixed before outcomes are seen, uncertainty and sensitivity, validation and a responsible-practice section. A coach reviews it at four milestones, a peer reviews the draft and a panel examines the result. Credit goes to the reasoning, not to a favourable answer.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Design a decision-grade analysis from a sponsor's question, fixing the estimand, decision rule and analysis plan before outcome data are examined.
- Diagnose data quality problems and their effect on the conclusion, and document the prepared data.
- Deliver the main analysis with a design matched to the question, whether experimental, quasi-experimental, model-based or Bayesian, and validate it.
- Quantify the uncertainty of the conclusion and test which assumptions would reverse the recommendation.
- Audit the analysis for privacy, subgroup effects and limits under the national rules.
- Defend the recommendation and its limits before a panel, re-running one result live.

## Topics

### Framing and planning

- Decision questions, estimands and decision rules
- Plans fixed before outcomes
- Scoping to eight weeks

### Evidence quality

- Data quality and its effect on conclusions
- Validation matched to the design
- Sensitivity and break-points

### Responsible practice

- Classification, minimisation and sharing
- Subgroup effects
- Disclosure of AI assistance

### Reporting and defence

- Decision briefs for non-specialists
- Reproducible repositories
- Answering a panel
