# USE-SEC-204 · AI in Education Systems

- Track: Data & AI Fluency
- Path: [AI in Sectors](../paths/USE-SEC.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Workshop: two days of labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map
- Total hours: 16 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Leaders who set policy, buy platforms and protect learners work on a fictional education directorate with synthetic and public data. They test a tutor for answer-giving and unsafe replies, check an essay scorer and an early-warning model by student group and draft integrity and safeguarding rules, ending with a defended opportunity-and-risk map. Classroom assessment is USE-EDU-201; building tutors is USE-EDU-204.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Distinguish learning-support, assessment, admission and administrative AI uses by who is affected.
- Test a tutoring assistant for answer-giving, accuracy and age-appropriate behaviour.
- Analyse agreement and error by student group for an essay scorer and an early-warning model.
- Draft an AI integrity and safeguarding policy covering minors' data, permitted uses, disclosure and incidents.
- Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking education use cases by learning value, data readiness and risk tier.

## Prerequisites

- [USE-FLU-103 · How AI Systems Learn and Where They Fail](USE-FLU-103.md)
- Leads or advises schools or universities

## Topics

### Uses

- Tutoring
- Scoring and feedback
- Admissions and early warning
- Teacher planning

### Learning effect

- Tutor mode versus answer mode
- Unguided help and unaided performance
- Pilots that measure learning

### Decisions and safeguarding

- Agreement with markers by group
- Grading controversies and appeals
- Minors' data and guardian consent
- Age-appropriate design and incidents
