## Description

Researchers in any discipline use AI on one synthetic project, a scoping question on heat exposure in outdoor workers with 200 retrieved records and a public dataset, from screening and summary checks to a reproducible analysis and a disclosure statement. Research practice for AI scientists is SPC-RSP-301.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Distinguish acceptable, disclosable and prohibited research uses of AI.
- Use scholarly databases and an AI screening assistant to map a literature with a recorded search.
- Test AI-written paper summaries for misreported populations, methods, effect sizes and conclusions.
- Implement an AI-assisted analysis from a pre-stated plan and reproduce it from a clean run.
- Redesign one research task with AI, measure it on two runs and disclose the AI use.

## Topics

### Integrity and disclosure

- Misconduct rules applied to AI
- Disclosure rules
- Confidential manuscripts and reviews

### Literature mapping

- Search strategies across databases
- Screening recall and stopping rules
- Checking references and retractions

### Analysis and writing

- Plan before prompt
- Forking paths at machine speed
- Drafting only from own outputs

### Data and participants

- Sensitive and government data
- Ethics approval conditions
