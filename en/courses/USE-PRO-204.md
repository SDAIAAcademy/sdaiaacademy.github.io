# USE-PRO-204 · AI for Legal, Regulatory and Compliance Work

- Track: Data & AI Fluency
- Path: [Fluency for Professions](../paths/USE-PRO.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline
- Total hours: 12 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Participants act as the legal team of a synthetic company engaging a data-processing supplier. With an assistant they research, review the contract against a playbook and map a regulation to obligations, checking every authority at source and deciding handling before documents enter a tool.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Identify how AI legal research fails: invented authorities, wrong jurisdiction, repealed provisions, misstated holdings.
- Apply a contract playbook with an assistant to flag deviations, missing clauses and changes between drafts.
- Prepare an obligations register mapping provisions to owners, controls and evidence.
- Classify matter documents for confidentiality, privilege and personal data, and choose the tool and redaction each allows.
- Redesign one recurring legal task and measure time and errors before and after.

## Prerequisites

- [USE-FLU-201 · Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own](USE-FLU-201.md)
- Reads contracts, regulations or policies at work and knows how laws are cited locally

## Topics

### Where legal AI fails

- Invented cases, articles and quotations
- Agreeing with a leading question

### Checking authorities and contracts

- Legislation portals and consolidated texts
- Authoritative versions and unofficial translations
- Playbook positions, fallbacks and red lines

### Regulatory mapping

- From provision to obligation
- Owners, controls and evidence

### Confidentiality and privilege

- Privilege and third-party tools
- Redaction and pseudonymisation
- Disclosing AI use to clients and courts
