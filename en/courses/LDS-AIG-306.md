# LDS-AIG-306 · Privacy, Intellectual Property and Content Provenance in AI

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [AI Governance, Risk and Assurance](../paths/LDS-AIG.md)
- Level: Level 300 · Develop
- Format: Workshop: one and a half days with labs and a follow-up review
- Total hours: 12 h
- Audience: specialist
- Status: In development

## Description

For a fictional publishing group, participants map personal data through a fine-tuning and retrieval pipeline, probe a small fine-tuned model for memorised records, answer a deletion request, decide which training sources may be used, and verify and strip content credentials on generated images. Data access design is LDS-DMG-301; model licences are LDS-AIG-203; privacy-enhancing technologies are DEV-SEC-303.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Prepare a personal data map of a fine-tuning and retrieval pipeline with lawful basis and transfers.
- Test a model for disclosure of planted records, with and without an output filter.
- Analyse a deletion request against weights, index, logs and backups.
- Decide for each training source whether to use, exclude or license it, and set output-use rules.
- Design a provenance and labelling scheme for generated media and test where it fails.

## Prerequisites

- [LDS-AIG-201 · AI Governance Foundations: Principles, Roles and Lifecycle Controls](LDS-AIG-201.md)
- Explains lawful basis and data subject rights (LDS-DMG-206 or equivalent)
- Runs provided notebook cells

## Topics

### Personal data in AI

- Training, retrieval, memory and logs
- Lawful basis and secondary use
- Sensitive data for bias testing
- External model services and transfer

### Leakage and rights

- Memorisation and extraction
- Output filters
- Deletion against weights; unlearning claims

### Intellectual property

- Text and data mining exceptions and rights reservations
- Output ownership and infringement

### Provenance

- Signed content credentials
- Removable watermarks
- Labels and deepfake rules
