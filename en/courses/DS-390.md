# DS-390 · Data Science Practicum

- Track: Data Science
- Path: [Data Science: courses that help prepare for the Associate level](../paths/DS-300.md)
- Level: Level 300 · Develop
- Format: Practicum: about eight weeks part-time, teams of three or four on a decision question set by a sponsoring government entity or company, with milestone reviews by experienced data scientists
- Total hours: 100 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Teams of three or four take one decision question from a sponsoring government entity or company, such as which cases to inspect first or whether a pilot worked, and carry it from framing to a recommendation the sponsor can act on: decision and metrics, data audit, an analysis plan fixed before outcomes are seen, validated analysis with stated uncertainty, and a briefing. Data are the sponsor's anonymised extract or a synthetic replica; when neither is approved in time, the team works on a prepared case from Sidra Freight, a fictional logistics company.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Prepare a framing memo that states a sponsor's request as a decision question with options, a success metric and constraints.
- Prepare a data audit covering provenance, quality, classification, personal data and fitness for the question.
- Design an analysis plan matched to the question type and fix it before outcomes are examined.
- Build and validate the analysis with baselines, sensitivity checks and stated uncertainty for every number the recommendation uses.
- Defend a recommendation, its limits and the evidence that would reverse it to the sponsor and a technical panel.
- Prepare a hand-over pack with a decision log, an analysis card and open risks.

## Prerequisites

- [DS-309 · Responsible Data Science: Privacy, Fairness and Documentation](DS-309.md)
- [DS-310 · Problem Framing and Analytical Review](DS-310.md)
- At least four further level 300 core courses, including DS-302 or DS-303
- About ten hours a week for eight weeks

## Topics

### Framing and data

- Stakeholder interviews and success metrics
- Sharing terms, classification and anonymisation
- Fitness of data for the question

### Analysis design

- Choosing a descriptive, predictive, causal or experimental design
- Plans and validation schemes fixed in advance

### Delivery under review

- Milestone and code reviews
- AI-assisted steps with verification notes

### Recommendation and hand-over

- Briefing a sponsor and answering challenge
- Decision logs, analysis cards and monitoring
