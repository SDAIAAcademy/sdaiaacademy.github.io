## Description

Working on a synthetic organisation's policy set, participants turn weak statements into owned, testable requirements traced to their sources, then draft, consult on and defend a generative AI use standard for staff. Rules for data used by AI systems belong to LDS-DMG-301.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Classify statements as policy, standard, procedure or guidance, and record gaps and conflicts.
- Draft testable requirements, each with one obligation, an owner and evidence of compliance.
- Prepare a traceability matrix from external instruments to internal requirements.
- Design an exception and review process with criteria, expiry, compensating controls and review triggers.
- Lead the consultation on a generative AI use standard and defend the revised text.

## Topics

### Document architecture

- Policy, standard, procedure, guideline
- Approval routes
- Document control: owner, version, review date

### Controls language

- Normative verbs, one obligation per sentence
- Measures, owners and evidence
- Words that defeat audit
- Bilingual drafting with a term base

### From source to control

- Reading instruments for obligations
- Traceability matrices
- Machine-readable control catalogues

### AI provisions and upkeep

- Prohibited uses and approved tools by data class
- Comment dispositions
- Exceptions that expire; review triggers
