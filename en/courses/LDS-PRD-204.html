LDS-PRD-204

Managing AI Projects and Delivery Teams

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 3 days · 16 h
  • Available

Participants steer delivery of an AI document-processing service for a fictional ministry whose target accuracy may be unreachable. They plan increments with exit criteria, assign roles, estimate in ranges, keep a risk log, decide gates on evidence and write the operations hand-over, while a simulation injects a model deprecation and a delayed data-sharing approval.

Under review before its first delivery

Who it is for

Audience: practitioner

Product managers and owners, service and interaction designers, and delivery leads who own AI features or services, and engineers moving into product roles.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Applied

    Prepare a plan of time-boxed increments, each with a hypothesis and an exit criterion.

  2. Applied

    Prepare range estimates, a responsibility matrix and a risk log covering model, data, vendor and adoption risks.

  3. Performance

    Decide go, no-go or pivot at delivery gates from evaluation, cost and risk evidence.

  4. Applied

    Draft an operations hand-over covering monitoring, model change, rollback and retirement.

More about this course

Topics

Delivering under uncertainty

  • Experiments before commitments
  • Evaluation cycles as the unit of progress
  • Stopping as a valid result; scaling is LDS-EXE-207

Teams and estimates

  • Roles, vendors and decision rights
  • Range estimates and reference classes
  • Backlogs that hold evaluation and data work

Risk and gates

  • Risk logs for AI work
  • Gates decided on evidence
  • Status reports that show uncertainty

Hand-over to operations

  • Service levels and monitoring
  • Model change and vendor deprecation
  • Rollback and retirement

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised check: risks and gate decision for an unseen status report (20%)
  • Graded labs and simulation (40%)
  • Delivery pack and defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall and at least 60% on the check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the supervised check and simulation gate decisions. AI-tutor in the planning labs. AI-full for drafting the hand-over, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
