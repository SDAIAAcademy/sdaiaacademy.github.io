## Description

A synthetic organisation is hiring an analyst and planning team learning. Participants prepare adverts, interviews and learning plans with an assistant and test it for bias with matched curricula vitae (CVs), under one rule: AI helps prepare, people decide about people.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Explain why ranking candidates with a general assistant is high-risk and which HR decisions stay with people.
- Analyse an AI-drafted job description for exclusionary wording and inflated requirements, and revise it.
- Test a screening prompt for bias with applications that differ only in name, gender or age.
- Prepare an interview guide and learning plans without passing identifiable data to unapproved tools.
- Redesign one recurring HR task and measure time, errors and fairness before and after.

## Topics

### HR tasks and where AI fits

- Decisions that stay with people
- Candidates' AI use and unreliable detectors

### Bias and screening risk

- How bias enters through data, wording and instructions
- Matched pairs and repeat runs
- Employment as a high-risk use
- Fairness in the redesign measure

- Explaining and contesting decisions
