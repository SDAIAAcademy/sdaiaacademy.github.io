# LDS-EXE-101 · AI for Executives: A Hands-On Briefing

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [Executive Leadership](../paths/LDS-EXE.md)
- Level: Level 100 · Explain
- Format: Primer: half a day, hands-on, with a scenario test
- Total hours: 4 h
- Audience: leadership
- Status: In development

## Description

Executives do one task from their own work with and without an assistant and check it claim by claim, watch an agent attempt two similar multi-step jobs and note where it fails, and leave with questions for any team proposing AI.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Describe where assistants and agents succeed and fail on similar tasks.
- Compare assisted and unassisted results on one task, checking facts, figures and sources.
- Prepare questions on task, evidence, data, cost, oversight and owner for an AI proposal.

## Prerequisites

- None; tracked pre-work: pick and classify one non-confidential task from own work

## Topics

### What current systems do

- Assistants, reasoning models, agents
- Uneven capability; partial credit is not finished work

### Reading the claims

- Benchmark and adoption denominators
- Self-reported versus measured productivity

### Questions for a team

- Evidence on own cases; cost per completed task
- Which data may enter which tool; who owns and stops it
- Use AI only where someone can challenge it
