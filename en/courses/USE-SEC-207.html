USE-SEC-207

AI in Justice and Legal Services

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 3 days · 16 h
  • Available

Managers from courts, prosecution, legal aid and legal departments measure error rates of research, contract review and triage tools on synthetic material, check an automated decision input for due process and build an opportunity-and-risk map for a synthetic justice ministry. Individual legal work with AI is USE-PRO-204.

Open the coursePractice questions

Under review before its first delivery

Who it is for

Audience: practitioner

Managers, specialists, regulators and advisers in health, energy, government, education, mobility, finance, justice, media, manufacturing, retail and telecommunications, agriculture and environment, and cities and tourism.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Distinguish legal AI uses that assist a lawyer from those that inform decisions about people.

  2. Applied

    Test research and contract review assistants for invented authorities and missed clauses against the sources.

  3. Applied

    Measure a triage classifier's errors at two thresholds and state who bears each.

  4. Applied

    Analyse an automated decision input for notice, explanation, human review and appeal.

  5. Performance

    Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking legal AI uses by value, error cost, data readiness and contestability.

More about this course

Topics

Uses

  • Research and drafting
  • Intake and triage
  • Contract review
  • Arabic transcription and translation

Errors that matter

  • Fabricated and outdated citations
  • AI-generated or altered evidence
  • Automation bias
  • Bias in historical case data

Due process

  • Notice and explanation
  • A decision-maker of record
  • Appeal and correction
  • Disclosing AI use to courts and clients
  • High-risk classification by jurisdiction

Mapping and pilots

  • Value, error cost and reversibility
  • Confidentiality and privilege
  • Pilots with a reviewed sample

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Assistant over provided documents
Open WebUI with an open-weight model, AnythingLLM, an approved enterprise assistant
Spreadsheet
LibreOffice Calc, Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets

How the work is assessed

  • AI-off scenario check, ten cases (25%)
  • Lab records (35%)
  • Map and five-minute defence (40%)
To pass
70% overall and 60% on the AI-off check

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the scenario check and for checking assistant output against sources. AI-tutor in the triage and due-process labs. AI-full for the map, defended live.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
