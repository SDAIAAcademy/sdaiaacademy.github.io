## Description

Participants use AI on commercial work for one synthetic home-appliance retailer, with a customer extract, 2,000 Arabic and English reviews and a campaign brief, keeping figures in code, every claim substantiated and every contact consented. Producing synthetic media is USE-FLU-206.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse customer feedback with an assistant into counted themes, validated against a hand-coded sample.
- Prepare segment notes from customer data and test them for unsupported generalisations and sensitive inferences.
- Draft a bilingual campaign with a register of the substantiation for each claim.
- Classify contacts by consent status and channel before personalised outreach.
- Redesign one recurring commercial task with AI and measure time and errors on two runs.

## Topics

### Where AI misleads here

- Insight that confirms the brief
- Invented market statistics and competitor facts

### Insight and segments

- Theme extraction and validation
- Dialect and mixed-language text
- Sensitive-trait inferences

### Campaigns and claims

- Claim types and substantiation
- Bilingual brand voice
- Labels and likeness rights for synthetic images

### Consent and customer data

- Marketing consent and opt-out
- Minimising customer data in prompts
