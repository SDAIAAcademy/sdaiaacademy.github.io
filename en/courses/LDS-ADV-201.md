# LDS-ADV-201 · AI Use-Case Feasibility and Business Cases

- Track: Executives & Leaders
- Path: [Advisory and Value](../paths/LDS-ADV.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Intensive: 3 teaching days and a capstone day, with guided pre-work and a tracked follow-up task
- Total hours: 24 h
- Audience: specialist
- Status: In development

## Description

Participants take one use case of a fictional municipal services agency through a feasibility study: a baseline from synthetic service logs, a data readiness score, and a timeboxed spike in which a retrieval-grounded prototype is measured on 30 labelled Arabic and English questions. They screen risk, compare four options including one without AI, and write a costed business case. Portfolio triage of many use cases is LDS-EXE-201.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Prepare a problem statement with a measured baseline, a value hypothesis and a success threshold.
- Analyse data readiness: coverage, quality, rights of use, classification and personal data.
- Test technical feasibility by adapting a retrieval-grounded prototype and measuring it on labelled questions.
- Classify the use case's risk level and identify the controls and human oversight it needs.
- Build a costed business case comparing four options, including one without AI, with sensitivity analysis.

## Topics

### Framing and value

- The decision behind the request
- Baselines from process data
- Value hypotheses and stop criteria

### Data readiness

- Coverage, quality and rights of use
- Classification and personal data
- Permissions that retrieval must respect

### Technical feasibility

- Designing a timeboxed spike
- Small labelled test sets, pass rates and their uncertainty
- Failure types, and when the answer is not AI

### Risk screening

- Risk levels and prohibited uses
- Effects on people and human oversight
- Vendor and cross-border data exposure

### Options and the costed case

- Change the process, buy, build or partner
- Total cost of ownership, including evaluation and human review
- Break-even, sensitivity and the recommendation
- Benefit tracking and review points after launch
