## Description

Participants hand multi-step work to an AI agent that acts on mail, files and calendars through connectors and on a schedule, and stay in charge. In a sandbox of synthetic data they brief the agent, grant permissions stepwise, gate irreversible actions, catch planted injections and review transcripts, then supervise a task of their own over two runs.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Prepare a delegation brief with goal, standing context, permitted sources, forbidden actions, stop conditions and result format.
- Configure least-privilege connector permissions and approval before every send, delete, payment or publication.
- Test an agent against hidden instructions and apply a stop, contain and report routine.
- Analyse agent transcripts for skipped steps, invented facts, actions beyond the brief and unsupported claims of success.
- Design and supervise an agent task from the participant's own work and decide to keep, change or stop it.

## Topics

### Choosing and briefing

- Chat, fixed workflow or agent
- Partial versus complete task success
- Standing instructions, project files and skills

### Permissions and approvals

- Read, write and act scopes
- Time-limited access and revocation
- Scheduled and background runs

### Security for the delegator

- Hidden instructions in mail, pages, files and images
- Untrusted content, private data and an outbound channel
- Poisoned memory and standing context

### Reviewing agent work

- Reading a transcript step by step
- Parallel agents and review time
- Records of who approved what
