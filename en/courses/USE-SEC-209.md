# USE-SEC-209 · AI in Manufacturing and Industry

- Track: Data & AI Fluency
- Path: [AI in Sectors](../paths/USE-SEC.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Workshop: two days of eight sessions with labs, tracked pre-work and a defended map
- Total hours: 16 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Plant, quality and maintenance managers score inspection and maintenance models by error cost on public industrial data, audit a quality dataset for readiness, review a robotics-cell change for safety and build an opportunity-and-risk map for a synthetic plant.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Describe the data, labels and owners that inspection, maintenance, optimisation and robotics cells need.
- Measure an inspection model's miss and false-reject rates and convert them into cost per thousand parts.
- Use a remaining-useful-life forecast to set a maintenance trigger and compare it with a fixed schedule.
- Analyse a quality dataset for missing defect classes, label disagreement and sensor drift.
- Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking plant AI uses by value, data readiness, safety impact and effort to scale.

## Prerequisites

- [USE-FLU-201 · Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own](USE-FLU-201.md)
- Knows how a production line, its quality checks and maintenance run; uses a spreadsheet

## Topics

### Uses

- Visual inspection and anomaly detection
- Predictive maintenance
- Process and energy optimisation
- Robotics cells and digital twins

### Quality data

- Rare defects and imbalance
- Inspector disagreement
- Drift, shifts and line changes

### Reading results

- Miss versus false-reject rates
- Operating points set by cost
- Edge or cloud inference at line speed

### Safety and security

- Functional safety and reassessment after changes
- Human stop authority
- Operational technology security
- Limits on monitoring workers
