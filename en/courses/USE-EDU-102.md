## Description

Learners see trial evidence that open assistants lower later unaided performance while a hint-only tutor does not. They compare answer and tutor modes, sort interaction patterns, set an assistant to tutor, and plan two weeks of AI-off-first practice with retrieval.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Distinguish interaction patterns that build understanding from those that replace it, citing trial evidence.
- Configure an assistant to give one hint at a time, ask for explain-back and withhold final answers.
- Prepare a two-week practice plan with AI-off first attempts, retrieval and spacing.

## Topics

### The evidence

- Practice gains versus unaided performance
- Why effortful practice feels worse

### Patterns that help and harm

- Delegation, repeated AI debugging
- Conceptual questions, generate-then-explain
- Polished answers get checked less

### Tutor mode

- Study modes and their limits
- Retrieval, spacing, disclosure
