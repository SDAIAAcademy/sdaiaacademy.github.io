SPC-RSP-302

Data Science Practicum

  • Level 300 · Develop
  • Practicum
  • 100 h
  • In development

Teams of three or four take a decision question from a sponsoring organisation, such as where to place a service, which cases to inspect first or whether a pilot worked, and carry it to a recommendation the sponsor can act on. They frame the decision, audit the data, fix the analysis plan before seeing outcomes, build and validate the analysis and defend it to the sponsor and an Academy panel. SPC-MOD-307 and SPC-ANL-301 are recommended beforehand.

Who it is for

Audience: specialist

AI research scientists, data scientists and research engineers who produce models, methods and evidence, and technical leads who must judge research claims.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Performance

    Design a decision question from a sponsor's request, with options, a success metric, constraints and the evidence that would change the decision.

  2. Applied

    Prepare a data audit covering provenance, quality, classification, personal data and fitness for the question.

  3. Performance

    Design an analysis plan matched to the question type (descriptive, predictive, causal or optimisation) and fix it before outcomes are examined.

  4. Performance

    Build and validate the analysis with baselines, sensitivity checks and stated uncertainty for every number the recommendation relies on.

  5. Performance

    Defend a recommendation and its limits to the sponsor and a technical panel, including what evidence would reverse it.

  6. Applied

    Prepare a hand-over pack with a decision log, an analysis card, a monitoring suggestion and open risks.

Format and days

Format
Practicum: about eight weeks part-time in teams of three or four, on a sponsor's decision question, with milestone reviews by experienced data scientists
Total hours
100 h

Prerequisites

  • SPC-STA-201 Statistical Inference by Simulation Level 200
  • SPC-MOD-201 Machine Learning for Tabular Data: Baselines and Validation Level 200
  • Writes analysis code in Python or R under version control
  • Has designed or analysed an experiment or a before-and-after comparison, as in SPC-EXP-201
  • Can commit about 12 hours a week for eight weeks

More about this course

Topics

Framing and scoping

  • Decision questions versus analysis requests
  • Stakeholder interviews and success metrics
  • Scope, timeline and team roles

Data access and audit

  • Sharing agreements and classification
  • Anonymisation and synthetic substitutes
  • Profiling and fitness for purpose

Analysis design and delivery

  • Matching method to question type
  • Validation schemes fixed in advance
  • Reproducible pipelines and code review
  • AI-assisted analysis with verification notes

Recommendation and hand-over

  • Uncertainty in plain words
  • Briefing a sponsor and answering challenge
  • Analysis cards, decision logs and monitoring

Tools and platforms

These are examples. Any equivalent tool may be used instead.

Analysis language and notebooks
Python with Jupyter, R with Quarto
Modelling and statistics libraries
scikit-learn, statsmodels
Data validation
pandera, Great Expectations, pointblank
Anonymisation and synthetic data
ARX, Synthetic Data Vault
Assistant approved for the sponsor's data
an open-weight model served locally, an approved hosted model

How the work is assessed

  • Five milestone reviews (30%)
  • Decision pack and repository scored on a six-criterion rubric (35%)
  • Sponsor briefing and panel defence (25%)
  • Individual oral on a selected component, without AI (10%)
To pass
70% overall, at least 60% on the defence, and a pass on the individual oral for every member

Use of AI in the course

AI-off in the individual oral. AI-full in team work within the sponsor's data rules: only approved tools touch sponsor data, and each assisted step carries a verification note.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
