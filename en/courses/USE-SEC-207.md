## Description

Managers from courts, prosecution, legal aid and legal departments measure error rates of research, contract review and triage tools on synthetic material, check an automated decision input for due process and build an opportunity-and-risk map for a synthetic justice ministry. Individual legal work with AI is USE-PRO-204.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Distinguish legal AI uses that assist a lawyer from those that inform decisions about people.
- Test research and contract review assistants for invented authorities and missed clauses against the sources.
- Measure a triage classifier's errors at two thresholds and state who bears each.
- Analyse an automated decision input for notice, explanation, human review and appeal.
- Build an opportunity-and-risk map ranking legal AI uses by value, error cost, data readiness and contestability.

## Topics

### Uses

- Research and drafting
- Intake and triage
- Contract review
- Arabic transcription and translation

### Errors that matter

- Fabricated and outdated citations
- AI-generated or altered evidence
- Automation bias
- Bias in historical case data

### Due process

- Notice and explanation
- A decision-maker of record
- Appeal and correction
- Disclosing AI use to courts and clients
- High-risk classification by jurisdiction

### Mapping and pilots

- Value, error cost and reversibility
- Confidentiality and privilege
- Pilots with a reviewed sample
