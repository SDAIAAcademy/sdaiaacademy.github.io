LDS-EXE-207

Scaling AI from Pilots to Production

  • Level 200 · Apply
  • Workshop
  • 3 days · 12 h
  • Available

Participants find why a fictional organisation's AI pilots stalled and plan how the best two reach production: they re-test one on realistic cases, score data readiness, choose an operating model, set scaling gates and redesign the process around one pilot so its value does not rest on workarounds.

Open the coursePractice questions

Under review before its first delivery

Who it is for

Audience: leadership

Board members, executives and senior managers in public and private organisations, and the strategy, finance, procurement, people and risk staff who prepare their AI decisions.

Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

  1. Awareness

    Identify why pilots stall: problem choice, data, integration, evaluation, ownership or unchanged processes.

  2. Applied

    Test a pilot's claimed performance on realistic cases against its demonstration.

  3. Applied

    Analyse two pilots' data readiness for access, quality, classification, lineage and refresh.

  4. Applied

    Compare central, federated and hub-and-spoke operating models for size, skills and risk.

  5. Performance

    Design scaling gates on evaluation results, governance approval, cost per successful task and process redesign.

More about this course

Topics

Why pilots stall

  • No path to production
  • Demonstration versus realistic cases
  • Ownership and funding after the pilot

Readiness

  • Data access, quality, classification, lineage
  • Integration with systems of record
  • Agent identity; run cost at volume

Operating model

  • Central, federated, hub-and-spoke
  • One owner for the path to production
  • Hand-over to operations (delivery is LDS-PRD-204)

Gates and redesign

  • Evaluation thresholds on realistic sets
  • Approval by risk level; monitoring, rollback
  • Redesign the process, not add a tool
  • Scale, fix or stop against a baseline

How the work is assessed

  • Supervised diagnosis of an unseen pilot, AI-off (25%)
  • Lab artefacts (30%)
  • Scaling plan and review (45%)
To pass
70% overall, at least 60% on the plan

Use of AI in the course

AI-off for the diagnosis; AI-tutor for readiness scoring; AI-full for the plan, defended in review.

AI-off: no AI assistance. AI-tutor: an assistant that hints and explains but does not give the answer. AI-full: any assistance; the participant owns the result and defends it.
