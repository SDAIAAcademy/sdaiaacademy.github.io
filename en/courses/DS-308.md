## Description

Participants turn a completed analysis into a recommendation a decision maker can act on. Analysts at Al-Nakhla Health Cluster, a fictional regional health provider, have finished a study of missed outpatient appointments and a text-reminder pilot. Participants frame the decision, build an answer-first storyline from fourteen findings of mixed value, redesign exploratory charts to explain, state uncertainty in plain Arabic and English, write a two-page decision memo and give an assessed live briefing to a panel playing the cluster's executives. Chart construction is DS-203.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Analyse a decision context to name the decision maker, options, criteria and the evidence that would change the choice.
- Draft an answer-first storyline that groups and orders the supporting reasons and discards weak findings.
- Prepare explanatory charts whose titles state the finding, annotated for one message in Arabic and English.
- Draft plain-language statements of uncertainty, assumptions and risks using ranges, natural frequencies and scenarios.
- Deliver a decision memo stating recommendation, evidence, uncertainty, alternatives and what to monitor.
- Defend a recommendation in a live briefing under questioning.

## Topics

### From analysis to recommendation

- Decision, options and criteria
- What the audience knows, needs and worries about
- From finding to claim to recommendation
- Selective reporting and misleading emphasis

### Structure and charts

- Answer first, then reasons
- Situation, complication, question, answer
- Titles that state the finding; bilingual layouts

### Uncertainty in plain words

- Ranges and natural frequencies
- How readers misread verbal probability terms
- What would change the conclusion

### Delivery

- Spoken structure and timing
- Saying what is not known
- Checking every number in AI-drafted material
