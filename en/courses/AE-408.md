## Description

Participants build and evaluate applications that see, listen and speak, with Arabic as the main case. For the fictional Heritage Trails Authority they extract grounded fields from visitor and ranger photos with vision-language models, benchmark open-weight speech recognisers on dialects and code-switched speech, test synthesised read-back, and assemble a bilingual voice booking line with turn-taking, interruption and tool calls within a latency budget. They test it with simulated callers and choose between open-weight and hosted, cascaded and speech-to-speech designs on measured quality, latency and cost. Photos are public-domain or synthetic; calls use synthetic voices and public speech corpora.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Implement image applications with vision-language models that return validated structured fields grounded in the image.
- Evaluate speech recognisers on Arabic dialects and code-switched speech with explicit normalisation rules.
- Build a bilingual voice agent that handles turn-taking, interruption and confirmed tool calls within a latency budget.
- Diagnose failure modes specific to image and audio inputs, including invented objects, text produced on silence and instructions hidden in images.
- Design a multimodal test set and an end-to-end evaluation with simulated callers.
- Decide between open-weight and hosted, cascaded and speech-to-speech designs on quality, latency and cost per call.

## Topics

### Vision-language models in applications

- Visual tokens, resolution and cost
- Structured extraction from photos
- Grounding with boxes and points
- Frames from video
- Instructions hidden in images

### Speech recognition

- Recogniser families and multilingual encoders
- Arabic normalisation of diacritics, letter variants and numerals
- Dialects, code-switching and telephone audio
- Streaming, voice activity detection, diarisation and text on silence

### Synthesis and voice agents

- Read-back of names, numbers and dates
- Cascaded, speech-to-speech and full-duplex designs
- End-of-turn detection and interruption
- Latency budgets and tool calls during speech

### Evaluation

- Multimodal test sets
- Error rates with intervals by slice
- Simulated callers
- Cost per call

### Responsible voice and image handling

- Voice and faces as personal data
- Consent, retention and minimisation
- Disclosure of synthetic voices
