## Description

Participants staff a synthetic municipal service centre where an automated agent answers first and hands cases to people. They draft grounded replies, route escalations including impersonation attempts and sample a week of conversations for quality.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Distinguish grounded from ungrounded replies and identify their failures, such as invented policies and promises staff cannot keep.
- Use a grounded assistant to draft courteous Arabic and English replies, checked against cited articles.
- Apply escalation rules to route complaints, vulnerable people, rights, safety, refunds above a limit and suspected impersonation.
- Measure reply quality by sampling, rubric scoring and error rates by category.
- Redesign one recurring service task and measure handling time, errors and escalations before and after.

## Topics

### Service work beside AI

- Handover from agent to person
- What staff own: promises, exceptions, apologies

### Grounding, tone and transparency

- Answering only from the knowledge base
- Stale articles and agreeing with a wrong premise
- Courtesy and dialect in Arabic and English
- Telling people when AI is involved

### Escalation and fraud

- Complaints, vulnerability, rights and safety
- Impersonation and cloned voices

### Quality sampling

- Sampling plans, rubrics and second raters
- Feeding fixes to the knowledge-base owner
