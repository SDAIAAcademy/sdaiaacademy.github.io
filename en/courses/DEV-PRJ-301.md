# DEV-PRJ-301 · AI Engineering Practicum

- Track: AI Engineering
- Path: [Practicums](../paths/DEV-PRJ.md)
- Level: Level 300 · Develop
- Format: Practicum: about eight weeks part-time in teams of three to five for a sponsoring organisation, with weekly engineer reviews, two gates and a sponsor defence
- Total hours: 100 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

Teams take one problem from a sponsoring government entity, company or non-profit to a shipped system in about eight weeks. They agree a specification and acceptance criteria with the sponsor, build in weekly iterations reviewed by experienced engineers, evaluate against the criteria, harden the system after another team attacks it, and hand it over with an operations plan. Sponsor data enters only as an anonymised extract or a synthetic replica of the sponsor's schema.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Draft a scoped specification with acceptance criteria, an initial evaluation set and an out-of-scope list.
- Prepare a data-handling plan that classifies the sponsor's data, replaces personal data and states what may reach which model.
- Build and ship a working AI system in weekly iterations with tests and human review of every merged change.
- Evaluate the system against the acceptance criteria with error analysis and a regression gate.
- Review the system's security with a threat model and another team's attack, and fix the highest risks.
- Deliver an operations plan and hand-over that the sponsor's team can run alone.

## Prerequisites

- DEV-LLM-201 and DEV-EVL-301, or has built and evaluated a language model application
- DEV-OPS-201, or has deployed a monitored container service
- DEV-SWE-201, or works in Git with coding agents, reviews and continuous integration

## Topics

### Engagement and scoping

- Sponsor agreements and intellectual property
- Discovery interviews and walk-throughs
- Acceptance criteria as the contract

### Team delivery

- Weekly iterations and demonstrations
- Coding agents with review proportionate to risk
- Tests, continuous integration and decision records

### Data and governance

- Classification and personal data
- Anonymised extracts and synthetic replicas
- Impact assessment and system card

### Quality and security

- Error analysis and regression gates
- Acceptance sessions with end users
- Threat modelling and a cross-team attack day

### Operations and hand-over

- Service objectives and monitoring
- Cost per successful task
- Runbooks, rollback and ownership
