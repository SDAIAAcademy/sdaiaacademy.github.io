# USE-PRO-201 · AI for Public Servants and Policy Work

- Track: Data & AI Fluency
- Path: [Fluency for Professions](../paths/USE-PRO.md)
- Level: Level 200 · Apply
- Format: Workshop: two days of labs on one synthetic case, plus a tracked baseline
- Total hours: 12 h
- Audience: practitioner
- Status: In development

## Description

A synthetic ministry is consulting the public on a new licensing rule. Participants do its drafting and analysis with an approved assistant, classifying material before it enters a tool and tracing every factual statement to an official source.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Identify AI drafting errors typical of government work: superseded rules, invented article numbers, misattributed positions.
- Classify case documents by sensitivity and match each to the tools its level allows.
- Prepare a consultation summary with theme counts that keeps minority positions and flags duplicate campaign comments.
- Draft an options brief and a citizen notice in which every factual statement cites an official source.
- Redesign one recurring task from the participant's work and measure time and errors before and after.

## Prerequisites

- [USE-FLU-201 · Working with AI Assistants: Delegate, Describe, Judge, Own](USE-FLU-201.md)
- Drafts official correspondence, briefs or minutes in current work

## Topics

### Policy work and its AI failure modes

- Superseded rules and unsourced figures
- Summaries that drop minority views
- Campaign comments and instructions hidden in submissions

### Checking against official sources

- Gazettes, legislation portals and statistics releases
- Effective dates and consolidated texts

### Government data rules

- Classification levels and approved tools
- Personal data in case files
- Freedom of information and disclosure of AI use
