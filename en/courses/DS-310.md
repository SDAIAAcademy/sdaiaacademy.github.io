## Description

Participants practise the work before and after an analysis, using requests from Ghadeer Water Company, a fictional water utility. They turn vague requests into decision questions with an estimand, a metric and a decision rule, interview a role-played manager, use domain knowledge of metering, billing and repair to see where the data mislead, and test whether data and deadline make the question answerable. They then review analyses by peers and by an assistant and write review notes ranked by consequence. Metric definition is DS-307; team review standards are DS-407.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, the participant can:

- Prepare a decision question with estimand, population, metric and decision rule from a vague request.
- Analyse a metric set for proxy failure and gaming, and propose guardrail metrics.
- Use domain documents and an expert interview to map how the data behind a metric are produced.
- Design an analysis plan stating data, method, threats to validity and the result that would change the decision.
- Review an analysis for errors in question fit, data handling, method, inference and communication.
- Decide whether a request needs description, an experiment, a causal analysis, a predictive model or no analysis.

### Day 2: Know the domain, plan the work

- How the data are made
- Metrics and proxies
- Analysis plans
- Threats to validity

### Day 3: Review others' work

- A review checklist
- Finding the flaws that matter
- Reviewing assistant-generated analysis
- Review notes people act on

### Day 4: Capstone: the permits backlog, framed and defended


## Topics

### From request to question

- Decision, estimand and population
- Descriptive, predictive and causal questions
- Process models such as CRISP-DM and their limits

### Stakeholders and domain knowledge

- Decision makers, users and affected groups
- Interviewing for constraints and evidence
- How operational processes produce data

### Metrics and plans

- Metric trees, proxies and Goodhart's law
- Plans fixed before outcomes are seen
- Threats to validity

### Analytical review

- A checklist from question to conclusion
- Ranking flaws by consequence
- Reviewing assistant-generated analyses
